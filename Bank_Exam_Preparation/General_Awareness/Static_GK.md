## 🏛️ 1. Indian History – Exam-Oriented Highlights

### 🔹 Ancient &amp; Medieval (Very Short)

- **Harappan / Indus Valley Civilization** – urban planning, drainage; sites: Harappa, Mohenjo-daro.
- **Maurya Empire** – Chandragupta Maurya, **Ashoka** (Kalinga war, Buddhism spread).
- **Gupta Period** – “Golden Age of India” (art, literature, science).

## 🗺️ 2. Indian Geography – Basics

- India location: **Northern &amp; Eastern Hemisphere**
- Tropic of Cancer passes through middle of India.
- Highest peak in India (in Indian territory): **K2 (Godwin Austen)**  
- Highest peak within Indian Himalayas (fully in India): **Kanchenjunga**
- Longest river in India: **Ganga** (Ganges)
- Largest state (area): **Rajasthan**
- Largest state (population): **Uttar Pradesh**
- Smallest state (area): **Goa**
- Union Territories examples: Delhi, Chandigarh, Puducherry, Ladakh, etc.

---

## 🏛️ 3. Indian Polity – Constitution Highlights

- Constitution came into force: **26 January 1950**
- “Father of Indian Constitution”: **Dr. B.R. Ambedkar**
- President = Head of State
- Prime Minister = Head of Government

### Important Posts (Basics)

- President of India – elected indirectly
- Vice-President – also ex-officio Chairman of Rajya Sabha
- Lok Sabha – House of the People (lower house)
- Rajya Sabha – Council of States (upper house)
- Governor – Head of State (in each state)

---

### Important Articles (Exam Favourites)

- **Article 14** – Equality before law  
- **Article 19** – Fundamental freedoms  
- **Article 21** – Right to life and personal liberty  
- **Article 32** – Right to Constitutional Remedies (Dr. Ambedkar called it “heart and soul of Constitution”)  

---

## 💹 4. Indian Economy – Basics

- India’s central bank: **RBI**  
- Planning body: **NITI Aayog** (replaced Planning Commission in 2015)
- GDP – Gross Domestic Product (total value of all final goods &amp; services produced within country in one year)

Key Sectors:

- **Primary** – Agriculture, mining
- **Secondary** – Manufacturing, industry
- **Tertiary** – Services (banking, IT, transport)

## 🧠 9. Practice Questions (with Answers)

```text
1)  When did India become a Republic?
2)  Who is known as the Father of the Indian Constitution?
3)  Which is the largest state of India by area?
4)  Which river is associated with the Sardar Sarovar Dam?
5)  Kaziranga National Park is famous for which animal?
6)  Which city is called the Pink City of India?
7)  Which Article of the Constitution is related to Right to Life?
8)  When is World Environment Day celebrated?
9)  NITI Aayog was formed in which year?
10) Which is the longest river in India?
```

---

### ✅ Answers

1) 26 January 1950  
2) Dr. B.R. Ambedkar  
3) Rajasthan  
4) Narmada  
5) One-horned rhinoceros  
6) Jaipur  
7) Article 21  
8) 5 June  
9) 2015  
10) Ganga (within India)
