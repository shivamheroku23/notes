## 🌟 1. Basic Banking Terms (Bilkul Simple)

### 🏛️ Bank

A bank is a place where:

- Log **paise rakhte** hain (deposit),
- Bank unhe **loan** deta hai,
- Aur beech ka kaam **safe keeping + services** hota hai.

---

### 💰 Deposit

Jab aap bank me paisa **rakhte** ho.

Types:

- **Savings Account** – normal public ke liye, thoda interest, withdrawal allowed.
- **Current Account** – business ke liye, zyada transactions, usually no interest.
- **Fixed Deposit (FD)** – fixed time ke liye lock, higher interest.
- **Recurring Deposit (RD)** – har month fixed amount deposit, maturity pe lump sum.

---

### 💸 Loan / Advance

Jab bank kisi ko paisa **udhaar** deta hai, interest ke saath.

Types:

- Personal loan
- Home loan
- Vehicle loan
- Business loan
- Overdraft / Cash Credit (for current account users/business)

---

## 🏦 2. Types of Banks in India

- **Public Sector Banks (PSB)** – Govt ka majority share (51%+)
  - SBI, Bank of Baroda, Punjab National Bank, etc.
- **Private Sector Banks** – Private promoters ka majority share
  - HDFC Bank, ICICI Bank, Axis Bank, etc.
- **Foreign Banks** – Head office outside India
  - HSBC, Citi, Standard Chartered, etc.
- **Regional Rural Banks (RRB)** – Rural area focus, jointly owned by
  - Central Govt, State Govt, Sponsor Bank
- **Co-operative Banks** – Members-owned, urban/rural co-op structure
- **Small Finance Banks** – Small borrowers, MSME, low income groups
- **Payments Banks** – Limited functions; small savings, payments, no large loans
  - e.g. India Post Payments Bank, Airtel Payments Bank

---

## 🧩 3. RBI – Reserve Bank of India

RBI = **Central Bank of India**  
Headquarters: Mumbai  
Established: **1935** (RBI Act, 1934)

### Main Functions:

- **Issue of currency notes** (₹2 and above – coins by Govt)
- **Banker to banks** – banks keep reserves with RBI
- **Banker to Government**
- **Controller of credit / monetary policy** – control inflation, liquidity
- **Foreign exchange management** (under FEMA)

---

## 📈 4. CRR, SLR, Repo, Reverse Repo (Very Important)

### 💧 CRR – Cash Reserve Ratio

- % of **Net Demand and Time Liabilities (NDTL)** that banks must keep with RBI in **cash**.
- No interest given on CRR balance.

👉 CRR ↑ → banks ke paas loan dene ke liye kam paisa → liquidity kam.

---

### 🏛️ SLR – Statutory Liquidity Ratio

- % of NDTL that banks must keep in form of **cash, gold, or approved securities** (like govt bonds).
- Maintained with bank itself.

---

### 🔁 Repo Rate

- Rate at which **RBI lends money to commercial banks** against government securities.
- Repo ↑ → loans costlier → inflation control.

---

### 🔄 Reverse Repo Rate

- Rate at which **RBI borrows money from banks**.
- Banks park extra funds with RBI at this rate.

---

> [!TIP] 💡  
> **Mnemonic**:  
> - **CRR** – Cash with RBI  
> - **SLR** – Securities with bank  
> - **REPO** – RBI gives money  
> - **Reverse REPO** – RBI takes money

---

## 🧾 5. NPA – Non-Performing Asset

- Jab borrower **interest/principal 90 days se zyada time tak nahi deta**,  
  loan **NPA** category me chala jata hai.

Types:

- Substandard
- Doubtful
- Loss assets

---

## 👤 6. KYC – Know Your Customer

- Bank customers ki identity verification process.
- Documents:
  - Aadhaar, PAN, Passport, Voter ID, Driving License, etc.

Purpose:

- Money Laundering rokna (AML)
- Terror funding control
- Fraud prevention

---

## 💳 7. Types of Cards &amp; Channels

- **Debit Card** – money directly from bank account
- **Credit Card** – bank se short-term loan
- **Prepaid Card / Wallet** – pre-loaded amount used
- **ATM** – cash withdrawal machine
- **POS** – point of sale swipe machine

---

## 🧮 8. NEFT, RTGS, IMPS, UPI

| System | Full Form                         | Use / Feature                              |
|--------|-----------------------------------|--------------------------------------------|
| NEFT   | National Electronic Funds Transfer| Batch based, any amount, slower            |
| RTGS   | Real Time Gross Settlement        | Real-time, big value (₹2 lakh+), one-to-one|
| IMPS   | Immediate Payment Service         | 24x7 instant, small-medium amounts         |
| UPI    | Unified Payments Interface        | Mobile-based instant payments using VPA    |

---

## 🏛️ 9. Important Banking Terms (Short List)

- **Repo Rate** – RBI → Bank lending rate
- **Bank Rate** – Long-term lending rate by RBI to banks
- **Base Rate / MCLR** – Minimum lending rate for banks (internal benchmark)
- **Priority Sector** – Sectors given priority in lending
  - Agriculture, MSME, Education, Housing (small), etc.
- **Financial Inclusion** – Bringing poor/unbanked into formal banking
- **PMJDY** – Pradhan Mantri Jan Dhan Yojana
