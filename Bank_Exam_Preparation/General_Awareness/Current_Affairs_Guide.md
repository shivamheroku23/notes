# 📰 Current Affairs Guide – What &amp; How to Study

> 🧠 Bank Mains mein **Current Affairs + Banking &amp; Economy** section sabse scoring hai  
> agar aap smart tareeke se padhai karo.

---

## 🌟 1. Current Affairs ka Scope (Kitna Cover Karna Hai?)

Exam date ke hisaab se:

- **Last 4–6 months** = must  
- Safe side: **6 months** (major news)
- Kuch important schemes / reports / appointments 8–10 months tak bhi pooch sakte hain.

---

## 📌 2. Kya-Kya Cheezen Important Hain?

### 2.1 Banking &amp; Economy

- RBI monetary policy:
  - Repo rate, Reverse repo, SLR, CRR changes
- Important committees of RBI/Govt
- Mergers of banks, new licences, guidelines
- Important schemes related to:
  - Financial inclusion
  - Digital payments
  - MSMEs, agriculture, etc.

---

### 2.2 National Affairs

- Major bills &amp; acts passed in Parliament
- Important govt schemes:
  - PMJDY, PM-KISAN, PMAY, Atmanirbhar Bharat, etc.
- Major national events (census, surveys, flagship missions)

---

### 2.3 International Affairs

- Important international summits:
  - G20, BRICS, SCO, UN meetings
- India’s role in international organizations:
  - UN, World Bank, IMF, WTO, WHO

---

### 2.4 Appointments &amp; Resignations

- New:
  - RBI Governor / Deputy Governors
  - Bank CMD/MD/CEO
  - CJI, CAG, Election Commissioner, Chief of Army/Navy/Air Force
  - Important international organisation heads

---

### 2.5 Ranks &amp; Reports

- India’s rank in:
  - Human Development Index
  - Global Hunger Index
  - Ease of Doing Business (till last published)
  - Corruption Perceptions Index
- Important reports by:
  - World Bank, IMF, UNDP, NITI Aayog, etc.

---

### 2.6 Awards &amp; Honours

- Bharat Ratna, Padma awards
- Nobel prizes
- Major sports awards (Arjuna, Khel Ratna)
- Film awards (National Film Awards, Oscars – basic level)

---

### 2.7 Sports

- Major tournaments:
  - Cricket World Cup, IPL winner
  - Olympics/Asian Games medals (if relevant year)
  - Wimbledon, French Open, etc. (winners list – only big ones)

---

### 2.8 Science &amp; Defence

- ISRO missions (Chandrayaan, Mangalyaan, etc.)
- DRDO important missiles/defence systems
- New defence deals, exercises (India+other country names)

---

## 📚 3. Sources – Kahan se Padhna?

- Reliable sources:
  - Standard monthly **Current Affairs PDFs** (Vision, AffairsCloud, etc.)
  - Monthly magazine form → **easy to revise**
- Daily:
  - News app / website se headlines padho
- Revision:
  - Weekly + monthly revision is must

> [!TIP] 💡  
> Sabse practical strategy:  
> - Roz full newspaper detail se padhne ki zaroorat nahi,  
> - **Monthly CA PDFs + revision** = enough for bank exams.

---

## 🧠 4. Study &amp; Revision Strategy

### Daily (30–45 min)

- 1 Daily CA PDF / app se:
  - Headlines + important details
- 10–15 MCQ questions solve

---

### Weekly

- Sunday ko:
  - Pichhle 7 din ka **Quick Revision**  
  - Important notes ko underline / highlight

---

### Monthly

- 1 Monthly CA PDF **2–3 baar** revise:
  - 1st read – rough reading + marking  
  - 2nd read – notes / starred points  
  - 3rd read – only starred points + MCQs

---

## 🧾 5. Notes Kaise Banaye? (Smart Notes)

Separate sections bana lo:

- Banking &amp; Economy
- Appointments
- Schemes
- Reports &amp; Indices
- Awards
- Sports
- National &amp; International

Har section me:

- Bullet points, short lines
- Example:

```text
Banking & Economy:
- RBI Monetary Policy (Month, Year):
  - Repo: x%, Reverse Repo: y%, CRR: z%, SLR: a%
- New scheme: XYZ – launched by which ministry, aim?
```

---

## 📝 6. Practice – Sample Current Affairs MCQs (Pattern Demo)

```text
1)  Who is the current Governor of RBI?
    (Options exam ke saal ke hisaab se honge.)

2)  Recently, which country hosted the G20 Summit?

3)  Which bank has recently merged with XYZ Bank?

4)  India’s rank in the latest Human Development Index is?

5)  Which Indian city was recently declared a UNESCO World Heritage Site?
```

(Real answers exam year pe depend karenge – isliye current data ke liye  
aapko latest CA PDFs follow karne honge.)

---

## 🎯 7. Attempt Strategy in Exam

- GA section is **not about calculation**; it’s about:
  - **Strong memory + smart selection**
- Exam mein:
  - Jo bhi question vague lagta ho ya jispe aapka confidence low ho → **skip/guess carefully**.
  - It’s better to attempt **80–90%** with good confidence than 100% random guessing.

---

> [!TIP] 🚀  
> Current Affairs ke liye mantra:
> - **“Read Less, Revise More”**  
> 4–6 months ke **quality notes** + multiple revisions  
> aapko GA section mein **bahut strong score** de sakte hain,  
> especially combined with Banking Awareness &amp; Static GK from is folder.
