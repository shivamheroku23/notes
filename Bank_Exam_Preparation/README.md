### 1.2 SBI PO – Exam Pattern (Overview)

Very similar to IBPS PO with minor variations:

- **Prelims:** 3 sections (Eng/Quant/Reasoning), 100 marks, 60 min
- **Mains:**  
  - Reasoning & Computer Aptitude  
  - Data Analysis & Interpretation  
  - General / Economy / Banking Awareness  
  - English Language (Objective + Descriptive)  
- Often **section-wise and overall cut-offs**; pattern may vary slightly year to year.

## 📅 3. 12-Week Master Plan (Prelims + Mains Foundation)

Assuming **working/college candidate, 3–4 hours/day on weekdays, 6–8 hours on weekends**.

### Phase 1 – Foundation (Weeks 1–4)

**Goal:** Clear basics in Quant, Reasoning, English + daily CA.

- **Daily (Mon–Fri):**
  - 1 hour – Quant basics (Number System, Percentage, Ratio, Averages)
  - 1 hour – Reasoning basics (Inequalities, Syllogism, Blood Relations)
  - 45 min – English (Grammar + Vocab + 1 RC)
  - 30 min – Current Affairs (last 6 months) – notes in Hindi-English mix
- **Weekend (Sat–Sun):**
  - 1 Full Prelims Mock (sectional timing) – detailed analysis
  - 2–3 hours – revise weak topics from mocks
  - 2 hours – General Awareness & Computer basics

### Phase 2 – Prelims-Oriented (Weeks 5–8)

**Goal:** Push speed + accuracy to Prelims level.

- **Daily:**
  - 45 min – Quant: Simplification, Approximation, Series, Quadratic, Easy DI
  - 45 min – Reasoning: Puzzles/Seating every day
  - 45 min – English: Cloze Test, Para Jumbles, Error Spotting
  - 30 min – Current Affairs & Banking Awareness
- **Alternate Days:**
  - 1 sectional mock (Quant/Reasoning/English)
- **Weekends:**
  - 2 full Prelims mocks (one each day)
  - Error log maintenance (Excel / notebook)

### Phase 3 – Mains-Oriented + Prelims Revision (Weeks 9–12)

**Goal:** Mains depth + maintain prelims sharpness.

- **Daily:**
  - 1 hour – Advanced DI & Arithmetic (caselets, data sufficiency)
  - 1 hour – High-level Reasoning (machine input-output, tough puzzles)
  - 45 min – English (Mains-level RC, inference, para completion)
  - 45 min – General/Economy/Banking Awareness (editorials, RBI reports, schemes)
- **Every 3rd Day:**
  - 1 Prelims-level mini mock (to retain speed)
- **Weekends:**
  - 1 Mains-level mock (sectional or full)
  - 1–2 hours – Descriptive practice (letter + essay)

> [!WARNING]
> Many students **ignore Mains until Prelims result**, losing 3–6 months. Start **Mains prep at least 8–10 weeks before Prelims**, especially for **GA & DI**.

## 🧱 4. Study Strategy – Topic & Subject Wise

### 4.1 Quantitative Aptitude

**Priority order (for most candidates):**

1. Number System, BODMAS, Simplification, Approximation  
2. Percentage, Ratio & Proportion, Averages, Profit & Loss, SI/CI  
3. Time & Work, Pipes & Cisterns, Time–Speed–Distance, Boats & Streams, Trains  
4. Mensuration, Permutation & Combination, Probability  
5. Data Interpretation (Tables, Line, Bar, Pie, Caselets, Missing DI)  
6. Data Sufficiency, Quantity Comparison

> [!TIP]
> Always connect **Arithmetic topics → DI**. Exams now often embed arithmetic inside caselets/DI rather than asking standalone sums.

### 4.2 Reasoning Ability

Core clusters:

- **High-frequency:** Inequality, Syllogism, Blood Relation, Direction & Distance, Coding-Decoding  
- **Scoring but time-taking:** Puzzles & Seating (Linear, Circular, Floor, Box, Day/Month-based)  
- **Mains-focused:** Machine Input-Output, Logical Reasoning, Data Sufficiency, Course of Action, Cause–Effect

### 4.3 English Language

- Vocabulary (roots, prefixes, suffixes, word families)  
- Grammar (subject-verb agreement, tenses, modifiers, prepositions, articles)  
- RC (fact-based, inference-based, tone, assumption)  
- Cloze Tests, Para Jumbles, Sentence Rearrangement, Error Spotting, Fillers, Word Swap

> [!NOTE]
> For bank exams, English is less about “high literature” and more about **applied comprehension + error-free usage**.

### 4.4 General Awareness

- Current Affairs of **last 6–8 months** – focus on:
  - Banking & Finance (rates, schemes, committees, RBI circulars)
  - Government schemes, budgets, important days, reports, indices
  - International news with economic impact
- Static GK:
  - Countries–Capitals–Currencies, National Parks, Dams, Stadiums, Airports, etc.
- Banking Awareness:
  - History & structure of RBI, commercial banks
  - NPA, SLR, CRR, repo-reverse repo, priority sector, Basel norms, NPCI products

### 4.5 Computer Knowledge

- Basics of Computer (input-output devices, memory types, number systems)  
- MS Office (Word, Excel, PowerPoint)  
- Internet & Networking basics (IP, URL, www, protocols)  
- Security (virus, malware, firewall, phishing)  
- Basic shortcuts and common software tools

## 🛠 6. How to Use Each Topic File

Every topic `.md` inside the subject folders will be structured as:

1. 🎯 Concept Map & Why It Matters  
2. 📚 Core Definitions & Full Forms  
3. 🧠 Deep Logic & Theory  
4. ⚡ Algorithms / Step-wise Templates  
5. 📝 Practice Questions (15–20 with solutions & analysis)  
6. 🕳️ Common Traps & Mistakes  
7. 📌 Micro-Revision Box  

Recommended usage:

1. **Read Sections 1–3 slowly** – understand logic, don’t memorize blindly.  
2. **Learn 1–2 main methods from Section 4**, then explore alternate methods.  
3. **Attempt practice questions in exam conditions** (timer on), then review step-by-step solutions.  
4. **Write your own ‘error log’** under Section 6 for each topic.  
5. **Revise Section 7** (Micro-Revision Box) **daily/weekly** until it becomes second nature.
