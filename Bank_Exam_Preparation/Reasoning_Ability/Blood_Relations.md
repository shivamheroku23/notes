# 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Blood Relations – Family Tree Logic

> 🧠 Is topic mein aapko family ke relations se related statements diye jaate hain,  
> aur poocha jaata hai ki X ka relation Y se kya hai?

---

## 🌟 1. Basic Relationship Words

- Father – pita  
- Mother – maa  
- Son – beta  
- Daughter – beti  
- Brother – bhai  
- Sister – behen  
- Husband – pati  
- Wife – patni  
- Uncle – chacha/mama/foofa (father/mother ka bhai ya relative)  
- Aunt – chachi/mausi/bua (father/mother ki behen ya bhabhi)  
- Nephew – bhatija/bhanja (brother/sister ka beta)  
- Niece – bhatiji/bhanji (brother/sister ki beti)  
- Cousin – chacha/mama/bua/massi ke bachche

---

## 🔑 2. Symbols for Easy Drawing

Use symbols:

- Male:  □  (square)  
- Female:  ○ (circle)  
- Marriage:  ——— between them  
- Children: vertical line downward

Example:

```text
   □———○
   |   |
  Son Daughter
```

---

## 🧮 3. Simple Example

> A is the brother of B. B is the mother of C. How is A related to C?

1. A – brother of B → A male, B sibling.  
2. B – mother of C → B female, parent of C.  
3. So A = brother of C’s mother → **maternal uncle**.

Answer: **Uncle**

---

## 🔁 4. “Pointing to a picture” Type

> Pointing to a photograph, A said, “He is the son of my father’s only son.”  
> How is the boy in photograph related to A?

Let’s decode:

- “my father’s only son” → A himself (if A is male)  
- So “son of my father’s only son” → A’s son.

Answer: **Son**

> [!TIP] 💡  
> Hamesha “my, his, her” ko step-by-step break karo. Pen se family tree draw karo.

---

## 🧪 5. Example – Mixed Relations

> B is the father of C. C is the sister of D. D is the mother of E. How is B related to E?

Stepwise:

- B – father of C → B male, C child.  
- C – sister of D → C &amp; D siblings, so D = child of B too.  
- D – mother of E → D female, E child.

So B = grandfather (D’s father and E’s grandfather).

✅ Answer: **Grandfather**

## 📝 6. Practice Questions (with Answers)

```text
1)  A is the brother of B. B is the sister of C. How is A related to C?

2)  Pointing to a woman, Ram said, “She is the wife of my mother’s son.”
    How is the woman related to Ram?

3)  P is the father of Q. Q is the brother of R. R is the mother of S.
    How is P related to S?

4)  A is the son of B. B is the daughter of C. C is the father of D.
    How is D related to A?

5)  Pointing to a man, a woman said, “His mother is the only daughter of my father.”
    How is the man related to the woman?
```

---

### ✅ Answers (Brief)

1. A – brother of B, B – sister of C → A and C are siblings.  
   A is **brother** of C.

2. “my mother’s son” → Ram himself (assuming only one son or Ram is that son).  
   Wife of my mother’s son → Ram’s wife.  
   So woman is **wife** of Ram.

3. P father of Q; Q brother of R → P father of R as well.  
   R mother of S → R female.  
   P = father of R → **maternal grandfather** of S.  
   So P is **grandfather** of S.

4. A son of B; B daughter of C; C father of D.  
   So D is child of C → B and D siblings.  
   A is B’s son → A is **nephew** of D (D is uncle/aunt of A).  
   D is **uncle/aunt** of A.

5. “my father’s only daughter” → the woman herself.  
   “His mother is the only daughter of my father” → his mother = woman.  
   So man is **son** of the woman.

> [!TIP] 🚀  
> Blood relation ke questions ko hamesha **diagram** se karo:  
> 1) Male/female symbol  
> 2) Generation level (grandparents → parents → children)  
> 3) “my, his, her” ko carefully follow karo.  
> 10–15 questions ke baad ye topic bahut easy lagne lagega.
