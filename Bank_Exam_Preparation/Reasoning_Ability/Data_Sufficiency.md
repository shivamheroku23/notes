# ✅ Data Sufficiency – Kya Itni Jaankari Kaafi Hai?

> 🧠 Is topic mein answer nikalna nahin hota, sirf yeh decide karna hota hai ki  
> **di gayi statements mein se kaunsi/kaun-si statements answer dene ke liye sufficient hain**.

---

## 🌟 1. Typical Instructions / Options

General pattern (may vary slightly by exam):

1. Statement I alone is sufficient, but Statement II alone is not sufficient.
2. Statement II alone is sufficient, but Statement I alone is not sufficient.
3. Both I and II together are sufficient, but neither alone is sufficient.
4. Either I or II alone is sufficient.
5. Neither I nor II are sufficient.

> [!NOTE] 📌  
> Data Sufficiency mein **exact value** nikalna zaroori nahi,  
> bas itna pata lagna chahiye ki nikal sakte ho ya nahi.

---

## 🧠 2. Approach (Very Simple)

For each question:

1. Sirf **Statement I** dekh ke check karo – kya answer definitely mil sakta hai?  
2. Sirf **Statement II** dekh ke check karo.  
3. Agar dono se alag–alag nahi milta, tab dono **together** check karo.  
4. Options ke hisaab se mark karo.

---

## 🧮 3. Example 1 – Age Based

**Q:** What is Rohan’s present age?

Statements:

I. Five years ago, Rohan was 10 years old.  
II. After 3 years, Rohan will be 18 years old.

### Check Statement I:

Five years ago age = 10 →  
Present age = 10 + 5 = 15 → Unique answer → **Sufficient**.

### Check Statement II:

After 3 years age = 18 →  
Present age = 18 − 3 = 15 → **Sufficient**.

Dono individually sufficient →  
Option: “Either I or II alone is sufficient”.

---

## 📘 4. Example 2 – Average

**Q:** What is the average of three numbers?

Statements:

I. The sum of the three numbers is 60.  
II. The largest number is 30.

- From I: sum = 60, but 3 numbers unknown → average = 60/3 = 20 → actually yahan number of terms known (3). So I alone → **Sufficient**.  
- From II: only largest known, others unknown → **Not sufficient**.

Right option: **Statement I alone sufficient**.

---

## 📏 5. Example 3 – Geometry

**Q:** What is the area of the rectangle?

Statements:

I. Length of the rectangle is 10 cm.  
II. Perimeter of the rectangle is 40 cm.

From II only:

Perimeter = 2(L + B) = 40 → L + B = 20.  
But Length and Breadth separately unknown.

- With I and II together:

L = 10, L + B = 20 → B = 10 → square.

Area = L×B = 100 cm²

So:

- I alone: **Not sufficient**  
- II alone: **Not sufficient**  
- I + II together: **Sufficient**

Right option: **Both together sufficient, neither alone.**

**Q:** What is the ratio of A’s salary to B’s salary?

I. A’s salary is ₹20,000.

**Q:** Who is the tallest among A, B, C?

I. A is taller than B.

---

## ✅ Answers &amp; Reasoning

### Q1

I. 2x + 3 = 11 → x = 4 → unique → **Sufficient**  
II. x² = 16 → x = +4 or −4 → two values → **Not sufficient**

Option: **Statement I alone sufficient**.

---

### Q2

I. Perimeter = 4×side = 40 → side = 10 → **Sufficient**  
II. Area = side² = 100 → side = 10 → **Sufficient**

Both individually sufficient →  
Option: **Either I or II alone sufficient**.

---

### Q3

Speed = distance / time

I. 150/3 = 50 km/h → **Sufficient**  
II. 200/4 = 50 km/h → **Sufficient**

Option: **Either I or II alone sufficient**.

---

### Q4

I. A salary = 20000 (B unknown) → Not sufficient  
II. B salary = 25000 (A unknown) → Not sufficient  
I + II: ratio = 20000 : 25000 = 4 : 5 → **Sufficient together**

Option: **Both together sufficient, neither alone**.

---

### Q5

I. A &gt; B → nothing about C → Not sufficient  
II. C &gt; B → nothing about A vs C → Not sufficient  
Together: A&gt;B, C&gt;B → still no relation A vs C → **Not sufficient**

Option: **Neither I nor II sufficient.**

---

> [!TIP] 🚀  
> Data Sufficiency mein galti tab hoti hai jab hum “shayad possible ho” ko “definite” maan lete hain.  
> Hamesha check karo – kya di gayi info se **har condition satisfy karte hue sirf ek hi answer aata hai?**  
> Agar multiple possibilities ho sakti hain → **not sufficient**.
