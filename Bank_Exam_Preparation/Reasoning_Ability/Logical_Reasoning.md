# 🧠 Logical Reasoning – Arguments, Assumptions, Conclusions

> 🧠 Ye part mainly **Mains** mein poocha jaata hai (statement–assumption, statement–conclusion, cause–effect, etc.).  
> Yaha calculation kam, **soch-zyaada** hoti hai.

---

## 🌟 1. Statement &amp; Assumption

- **Statement**: Given sentence (koi claim/baat).  
- **Assumption**: Wo baat jo **implicitly (andaz se)** maani gayi hai, par clearly likhi nahi hai.

### Example

Statement:  
> “The government should open more government schools in rural areas.”

Assumptions:

1. Many rural children **do not have access** to schools.  
2. Private schools are **not sufficient** or **too costly**.

Exam mein aapko options milte hain:

(A) (1) is implicit  
(B) (2) is implicit  
(C) Both  
(D) Neither

> [!TIP] 💡  
> Assumption = “For the statement to make sense, kya baat pehle se true mani gayi hai?”

---

## 📌 2. Statement &amp; Conclusion

- **Conclusion**: Jo directly / indirectly statement se **follow** karta hai.

Example:

Statement:  
> All employees in this office work for at least 8 hours a day.

Possible conclusions:

1. Some employees work for more than 8 hours.  
2. No employee works less than 8 hours.

- 2 → direct from statement → TRUE conclusion.  
- 1 → “at least 8” includes >8, but “some” not explicitly given; but logically, “at least 8” allows possibility of all exactly 8 also. So “some work &gt;8” **not definite**.

---

## 🌧️ 3. Cause &amp; Effect

Aapko 2 statements diye jaate hain, poochte hain:

- Kya **Statement 1 → cause**, Statement 2 → effect?  
- Ya independent hain?

Example:

1) It rained heavily in the city last night.  
2) Many roads in the city are now flooded.

Clearly:

- Cause: heavy rain  
- Effect: flooded roads

---

## 🧪 4. Small Practice Examples (with Answers)

### Q1 – Assumption

Statement:  
> “The company has decided to give training to all its sales staff.”

Assumptions:

I. The sales staff needs training.  
II. Training will improve their performance.

Which is implicit?

- If company training de rahi, wo ye maan rahi hai ki:
  - Staff ko training ki zaroorat hai → I implicit.  
  - Training se performance better hoga → II mostly implicit in such exam questions.

✅ Answer: **Both I and II implicit.**

---

### Q2 – Conclusion

Statement:  
> “All students in the class passed the exam.”

Conclusions:

I. No student failed.  
II. Some students scored full marks.

- I → correct: if all passed, none failed.  
- II → not given.

✅ Only I follows.

---

### Q3 – Cause &amp; Effect

Statements:

I. The prices of petrol have increased.  
II. People have started using public transport more frequently.

Here:

- Petrol price increase (I) → cause  
- More public transport usage (II) → effect

✅ I is the cause and II is the effect.
