# 🧩 Puzzles – Floor, Box, Scheduling (Step-by-Step)

> 🧠 Puzzles = big information + small clues.  
> Bank exams mein **high weightage**: Prelims + Mains.

---

## 🌟 1. Types of Puzzles

- Floor-based (floors of a building)
- Box-based (boxes stacked one above another)
- Scheduling (days, months, time slots)
- Misc: age, rank, blood relation mixed

---

## 🔑 2. General Strategy (Seedha Simple)

1. **Information table/diagram banao**
   - Floors: draw floors from **top to bottom** (8,7,6…1)
   - Boxes: topmost to bottommost levels
2. **Fix definite information first**
   - “A lives on 5th floor” → directly place.
3. **Then use negative clues**
   - “B does not live on top floor.”
4. **Use cross table / possibility method**
   - Jab direct nahi milta, 2–3 possibilities banao.
5. **Cross-check every new placement** with all clues.

> [!TIP] 💡  
> Never try to solve only mentally. Always draw a neat structure – speed + accuracy dono badte hain.

## 🏢 3. Floor-Based Puzzle – Example

### Question

There is a building with **5 floors**, numbered 1 (lowest) to 5 (topmost).  
Five persons – A, B, C, D and E – live on different floors.

Clues:

1. A lives on the **3rd floor**.  
2. C lives on a floor **above A**.  
3. E does not live on the **top floor**.  
4. B lives on the **1st floor**.  
5. D lives on a floor **below C**, but **above A**.

### Step-by-Step Solution

Draw floors:

```text
5:  _
4:  _
3:  _
2:  _
1:  _
```

From (1): A on 3rd

```text
5:  _
4:  _
3:  A
2:  _
1:  _
```

From (4): B on 1st

```text
5:  _
4:  _
3:  A
2:  _
1:  B
```

From (2): C is above A → floor 4 or 5.

From (5): D is below C but above A:

- So D must be on **4th** and C on **5th**  
  (Check: if C on 4, no floor between A (3) and C (4) for D)

Thus:

```text
5:  C
4:  D
3:  A
2:  _
1:  B
```

Remaining person: E on 2nd (and clue 3: not on top floor is satisfied).

Final:

- 5: C
- 4: D
- 3: A
- 2: E
- 1: B

## 📦 4. Box-Based Puzzle – Example

### Question

There are **5 boxes** – P, Q, R, S, T – kept one above another.  
Bottommost is position 1, topmost is 5.

Clues:

1. P is kept just **above** R.  
2. Only one box is between P and Q.  
3. S is not at the top.  
4. T is above Q but not at the top.

### Step-by-Step

Positions: 5 (top) to 1 (bottom)

We know “P just above R” → pair: PR (P on top of R).

Possible places for PR pair:

- (2,1), (3,2), (4,3), (5,4)

Try possibility:

**Case 1:** PR at (2,1):

```text
5: _
4: _
3: _
2: P
1: R
```

Clue 2: one box between P and Q:

- If P at 2, then Q at 4 (since 3 is between).
Layout:

```text
5: _
4: Q
3: _
2: P
1: R
```

Clue 4: T above Q but not at top:

- Above Q (4) only 5 possible. T at 5.  
- And S is not at top, so S at remaining 3.

Final:

```text
5: T
4: Q
3: S
2: P
1: R
```

Sab clues satisfy → yehi answer.

## 🗓️ 5. Scheduling Puzzle – Example (Days of Week)

### Question

Four friends – A, B, C, D – have exams on different days of a week from **Monday to Thursday**.  
Each one has exactly one exam.

Clues:

1. A’s exam is **before** C’s exam.  
2. B’s exam is on **Tuesday**.  
3. D’s exam is not on Monday.  
4. C’s exam is **after Wednesday**.

Days: Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday.

From (2): B → Tuesday.

From (4): C after Wednesday → C = Thursday only.

From (1): A before C:

- A must be on Monday or Tuesday.  
- But Tuesday already B → So A = Monday.

Remaining D → Wednesday (since not Monday).

Final:

- Monday: A  
- Tuesday: B  
- Wednesday: D  
- Thursday: C

## 🧠 6. General Puzzle Tips

- Name ke initials likho (A,B,C…) to save time.
- Clues ko number do, used ones ko tick karo.
- Jab bhi contradiction aaye → wo possibility reject karo.

## 📝 7. Practice Puzzle (Floor-Based) – Try Yourself

**Q:** 6-floor building, floors 1 (bottom) to 6 (top).  
Six persons – P, Q, R, S, T, U – live on different floors.

Clues:

1. P lives on an even-numbered floor.  
2. Only one person lives between P and Q.  
3. R lives **just above** T.  
4. S lives on the top floor.  
5. U lives on floor 1.  
6. Q does not live on floor 3.

🧩 Find the floor of each person.

---

### ✅ Answer (Outline)

Floors:

- 6: S  
- 5: Q  
- 4: P  
- 3: R  
- 2: T  
- 1: U  

(You can try to solve step-by-step like previous examples and then verify.)
