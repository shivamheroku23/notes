# 🧭 Direction Sense – Simple Movement &amp; Turning

> 🧠 Is topic mein person ka movement, turns, direction &amp; distance poocha jaata hai.  
> Agar basics clear hain to ye bahut scoring hai.

---

## 🌟 1. Basic Directions

Imagine a simple map:

```text
        North
          ↑
West  ←   +   →  East
          ↓
        South
```

---

## 🔁 2. Left / Right Turns

Facing **North**:

- Left → West  
- Right → East

Facing **East**:

- Left → North  
- Right → South

Facing **South**:

- Left → East  
- Right → West

Facing **West**:

- Left → South  
- Right → North

> [!TIP] 💡  
> Hamesha pehle person ki **current direction** likho, phir left/right decide karo.

---

## 🧮 3. Simple Example

> A man is facing North. He turns right, then right again, then left. Which direction is he facing now?

Step:

- Start: North  
- Turn right → East  
- Turn right → South  
- Turn left → East

✅ Final direction: **East**

---

## 📏 4. Distance &amp; Final Position

Example:

> Ram walks 4 km towards North, then 3 km towards East. How far is he from starting point?

Draw L-shape:

- Up 4, then right 3 → right triangle.

Distance formula (Pythagoras):

```text
Distance = √(4² + 3²) = √(16 + 9) = √25 = 5 km
```

✅ 5 km

---

## 🧪 5. Example – Direction from One Point to Another

> A walks 5 km North, then 5 km East. In which direction is A from starting point?

Diagram: Up 5, right 5 → diagonal.

From starting point, A is **North-East**.

---

## 📝 6. Practice Questions (with Answers)

```text
1)  A man is facing East. He turns left, then left again, then right.
    Which direction is he facing now?

2)  A walks 3 km North, then 4 km East. How far is he from starting point?

3)  A walks 6 km South, then 8 km West. How far is he from starting point?

4)  A walks 4 km North, then 3 km East, then 4 km South.
    In which direction is he from starting point?

5)  A walks 5 km East, then 5 km North, then 5 km West.
    How far is he from starting point and in which direction?
```

---

### ✅ Answers (Brief)

1. Start East:  
   Left → North  
   Left → West  
   Right → North  
   Final: **North**

2. Distance = √(3²+4²) = 5 km

3. Distance = √(6²+8²) = √(36+64)=√100=10 km

4. Path:  
   - 4 North, 4 South → vertically cancel except 0  
   - 3 East remains  
   So final = 3 km **East** of starting point.

5. Path:  
   - 5 East, 5 West → cancels  
   - 5 North remains  
   So 5 km **North** of starting point.
