# ❌ Error Spotting – Grammar Mistake Pakdo, Marks Kamao

> 🧠 Bank exams mein Error Spotting = **high-scoring** topic.  
> Aapko ek sentence diya hota hai, usko 4–5 parts mein baanta jaata hai.  
> Galti jis part mein hai, woh option answer hota hai.

---

## 🌟 1. Question Pattern (IBPS/SBI Style)

Typical format:

> Identify the part of the sentence that has an error. If there is no error, choose “No error”.

```text
A) He do not / B) know to / C) operate the / D) new machine. / E) No error
```

Yahan galti part A mein hai: “He **does** not” hona chahiye.

---

## 🧱 2. Sabse Important Grammar Areas (for Errors)

Error spotting mein mostly in topics se questions aate hain:

1. **Subject–Verb Agreement**
2. **Tense &amp; Verb Form**
3. **Articles (a, an, the)**
4. **Prepositions (in, at, on, by, for, to, etc.)**
5. **Pronouns (he, they, it, each, everyone, etc.)**
6. **Comparisons (than, as…as)**
7. **Modifiers &amp; Position of words**
8. **Parallelism (same pattern in list)**

Neeche har ek ka chhota summary + exam-level examples hai.

## 3️⃣ Subject–Verb Agreement (Singular–Plural Matching)

### Rule 1: Singular subject → Singular verb (V+s/es)  
Plural subject → Plural verb (base verb)

- He **plays**.
- They **play**.

❌ Error example:

> He **play** cricket every Sunday.

✅ Correction: He **plays** cricket every Sunday.

---

### Rule 2: “and” joins two singular → plural

- Ram and Shyam **are** friends. ✅

---

### Rule 3: “Each / Every / Everyone / Everyone of / Either / Neither” → singular verb

- Each student **was** present. ✅
- Neither of them **is** ready. ✅

❌ Error:

> Each of the players **are** ready.

✅ Correction: **is** ready.

---

## 4️⃣ Tense &amp; Verb Form

### Rule 1: Do/Does/Did + Verb (1st form)

- He **does not know**.
- They **do not play**.
- I **did not go**.

❌ Error:

> She did not **went** to school.

✅ Correction: did not **go**

---

### Rule 2: Has/Have/Had + Verb (3rd form – V3)

- He has **finished**.
- They have **gone**.
- She had **written**.

❌ Error:

> He has **gave** me the pen.

✅ Correction: has **given**

---

## 5️⃣ Articles – a / an / the

### Rule 1: “a” before consonant sound, “an” before vowel sound

- a book, a university (u = yu sound)
- an apple, an honest man (h silent)

❌ Error:

> He is **an European**.

✅ Correction: a European

---

### Rule 2: Use “the” for:

- Unique things: the sun, the moon
- Superlative: the best, the tallest
- Specific noun: the boy in red shirt

❌ Error:

> She is **best** student in the class.

✅ Correction: the best student

---

## 6️⃣ Prepositions (Common Errors)

- **Discuss** something (not “discuss about”)  
- **Describe** something (not “describe about”)  
- Different **from**, not different than (formal)

❌ Error:

> They discussed **about** the plan.

✅ Correction: discussed the plan.

---

## 7️⃣ Pronoun Errors

- Everyone / Each / Either / Neither → use **his / her**, not “their” (formal exam English).

❌ Error:

> Everyone should do **their** duty.

✅ Correction (formal): Everyone should do **his/her** duty.

---

## 8️⃣ Comparison Errors

- Use **than** for comparison:
  - better than, higher than, more than
- After “than”, usually same type of noun/pronoun expected.

❌ Error:

> She is taller than **me**.

Formal correction: taller than **I** (am).  
(Exam level: mostly they accept “me”, but for error spotting they may test “I”.)

---

## 🧠 9. Memory Tricks

- **“Does/Did + V1 only”** – kabhi V2 mat lagao.
- **Each/Every → singular** verb + singular pronoun (formal).
- **Superlative ke saath hamesha “the”** lagta hai.
- “Discuss about”, “describe about” → **always wrong**.

---

## 🧪 10. Solved Error Spotting Examples

### Example 1

> He do not / know how to / operate this / complicated machine. / No error

Error: “He do not” ❌

✅ Correct: He **does not** know…

So error part: **A**

---

### Example 2

> Each of the / players are / ready to / join the team. / No error

Error: “players are” with “Each” ❌

✅ Correct: Each of the players **is** ready…

Error part: **B**

---

### Example 3

> She has / completed / the project / yesterday. / No error

Tense mismatch – “has completed” with “yesterday” ❌  

Better: She **completed** the project yesterday.

Error part: **A/B/C** combined – exam mein mostly “has” wale part ko error mark karenge, so **A**.

---

### Example 4

> They discussed about / the new policy / in the meeting / yesterday. / No error

“Discussed about” ❌

✅ “discussed the new policy”

Error part: **A**

---

### Example 5

> Everyone should / bring their / own lunch / for the trip. / No error

Formal grammar: “Everyone” → singular → his/her  
Error part: **B**

✅ Everyone should bring **his/her** own lunch…

## 📝 11. Practice Questions (with Answers)

Mark the part with error (A/B/C/D) or E if “No error”.

```text
1)  A) He does not / B) knows how / C) to swim / D) properly. / E) No error

2)  A) One of the / B) boys have / C) not submitted / D) the homework. / E) No error

3)  A) She is / B) senior than / C) me by / D) three years. / E) No error

4)  A) The students / B) discussed about / C) the problem / D) in detail. / E) No error

5)  A) Neither of the / B) answers are / C) correct in / D) this question. / E) No error

6)  A) He has / B) finished / C) his work / D) yesterday. / E) No error

7)  A) This is / B) the more / C) beautiful place / D) I have seen. / E) No error

8)  A) Each of the girls / B) were given / C) a gift / D) by the teacher. / E) No error

9)  A) She prefers / B) tea than / C) coffee / D) in the morning. / E) No error

10) A) The manager / B) as well as the staff / C) are present / D) in the meeting. / E) No error
```

---

### ✅ Answers &amp; Explanations

1. “does not knows” ❌ → does not **know**  
   Error: **B**

2. “One of the boys have” ❌ → boys **has** (because “one” is subject)  
   Error: **B**

3. “senior than” ❌ → senior **to**  
   Error: **B**

4. “discussed about” ❌ → discussed the problem  
   Error: **B**

5. “Neither … are” ❌ → Neither … **is**  
   Error: **B**

6. “has finished … yesterday” ❌ → use **finished** only  
   Error: **A** (or A+D combo, but exam mein A)

7. “the more beautiful” ❌ → the **most** beautiful  
   Error: **B**

8. “Each … were” ❌ → Each … **was**  
   Error: **B**

9. “prefers tea than” ❌ → prefers tea **to** coffee  
   Error: **B**

10. “The manager as well as the staff are” ❌  
    Main subject = manager (singular) → verb singular  
    Correct: **is** present  
    Error: **C**
