# 📖 Reading Comprehension (RC) – Fast &amp; Accurate

> 🧠 RC = Passage + Questions.  
> Bank exams mein English ka **sabse important** part.

---

## 🌟 1. RC Question Types

1. **Direct (factual)** – line se seedha answer milta hai
2. **Inference** – line ko samajhkar hidden meaning nikalna
3. **Vocabulary** – word ka meaning / synonym / antonym
4. **Title / Main idea** – passage ka headline kya ho sakta hai
5. **True/False / Assertion-based**

---

## 🔑 2. Strategy for RC

### Step 1 – Skim the Passage

- Pehle 30–40 seconds mein **jaldi read**:
  - Topic kya hai?
  - Writer positive / negative / neutral?
- Har paragraph ka **1–2 word note** bana sakte ho (mentally):
  - Para 1 – Introduction
  - Para 2 – Problem
  - Para 3 – Solution

### Step 2 – Questions Dekho

- Ab questions read karo, options quickly.
- Phir passage ke relevant part pe wapas jao.

### Step 3 – Line-based Answer

- Answer hamesha **passage ke basis par** do.
- Apna general knowledge mat lagao; sirf passage se jo samajh aata hai.

> [!TIP] 💡  
> “Author ke dimaag se socho, apne dimaag se nahi.”

---

## 📘 3. Types of Questions – Examples

### 3.1 Direct Question

> According to the passage, what is the main cause of pollution in cities?

Answer: line mein direct written hoga.

---

### 3.2 Inference Question

> From the passage, it can be inferred that the author is:
> (A) optimistic  
> (B) worried  
> (C) angry  
> (D) bored

Yahaan aapko author ka **tone** samajhna hai.

---

### 3.3 Vocabulary in Context

> What is the meaning of the word “adverse” as used in the passage?

Options: negative, helpful, neutral, etc.  
Choose jo **context mein fit ho**.

## 🧪 4. Mini RC Example (With Questions)

### Passage

> Exercise plays a very important role in keeping our body healthy. In modern times, many people work in offices where they sit for long hours. This lack of physical activity leads to several health problems such as obesity, diabetes and heart diseases. Doctors advise that people should do at least 30 minutes of physical exercise every day. Even simple activities like walking, climbing stairs and doing household work can be beneficial.

---

### Questions

1. What is the main idea of the passage?  
   A) Office work is harmful  
   B) Exercise is important for health  
   C) Doctors do not like office workers  
   D) Walking is the best exercise

2. According to the passage, what causes health problems?  
   A) Eating too much food  
   B) Lack of physical activity  
   C) Going to office  
   D) Doing household work

3. Which of the following is **not mentioned** as a health problem?  
   A) Obesity  
   B) Cancer  
   C) Diabetes  
   D) Heart diseases

4. How much daily exercise do doctors advise?  
   A) 10 minutes  
   B) 20 minutes  
   C) 30 minutes  
   D) 60 minutes

5. Which of the following activities is **considered beneficial** in the passage?  
   A) Watching TV  
   B) Playing video games  
   C) Walking and climbing stairs  
   D) Sleeping for long hours

---

### ✅ Answers &amp; Explanation

1. **B) Exercise is important for health**  
   – Entire passage isi baat par hai.

2. **B) Lack of physical activity**  
   – Direct line: “This lack of physical activity leads to several health problems…”

3. **B) Cancer**  
   – Baaki teen directly mentioned.

4. **C) 30 minutes**  
   – Direct line: “at least 30 minutes…”

5. **C) Walking and climbing stairs**  
   – Directly mentioned as beneficial.

---

## 🧠 5. RC Speed &amp; Accuracy Tips

- Daily **10–15 mins English reading** habit:
  - newspaper editorial, business page, The Hindu, Indian Express, etc.
- Try to read with target:
  - “Main har paragraph ka 1–2 line mein meaning samjhunga.”
- Practice:
  - 1–2 RC sets daily (Prelims level)
  - Weekends pe 1 tough / Mains-level RC (longer, inference-based)

## 📝 6. Practice RC (Short) – With Questions

### Passage

> In many cities, air pollution has reached dangerous levels. The main sources of this pollution are vehicles, factories and construction activities. Polluted air can cause breathing problems, allergies and even serious lung diseases. Governments are trying to control pollution by introducing stricter laws and encouraging the use of public transport and cleaner fuels. However, citizens also have a responsibility to protect the environment by using cars less, planting trees and avoiding burning waste.

### Questions

1. What is the main topic of the passage?  
   A) Traffic problems  
   B) Air pollution in cities  
   C) Lung diseases in villages  
   D) Construction of roads

2. Which of the following is **not** a source of air pollution mentioned in the passage?  
   A) Vehicles  
   B) Factories  
   C) Burning waste  
   D) Construction activities

3. Which of these is mentioned as an effect of polluted air?  
   A) Eye blindness  
   B) Breathing problems  
   C) Heart attack  
   D) Skin cancer

4. What is the government doing to control pollution?  
   A) Planting trees only  
   B) Encouraging use of public transport  
   C) Encouraging use of more cars  
   D) Stopping construction

5. According to the passage, what should citizens do?  
   A) Use cars more  
   B) Burn waste regularly  
   C) Avoid planting trees  
   D) Use cars less and plant trees

---

### ✅ Answers

1. **B) Air pollution in cities**  
2. **C) Burning waste** (mentioned later as what citizens should avoid, not as main source)  
3. **B) Breathing problems**  
4. **B) Encouraging use of public transport**  
5. **D) Use cars less and plant trees**
