# 📘 Grammar Rules – Bank Exam Essentials

> 🧠 English grammar poora nahi, sirf **exam mein poochhe jaane wale** rules par focus.  
> Ye rules Error Spotting, Sentence Improvement, Fillers, Cloze sab mein kaam aate hain.

---

## 🌟 1. Parts of Speech (Very Short)

1. **Noun** – name of person/place/thing (Ram, city, book)
2. **Pronoun** – takes place of noun (he, she, it, they)
3. **Verb** – action/state (go, eat, is, have)
4. **Adjective** – describes noun (good, tall, red)
5. **Adverb** – describes verb/adjective (slowly, very)
6. **Preposition** – relation (in, on, at, by, for, to)
7. **Conjunction** – joining words (and, but, or, because)
8. **Interjection** – sudden feeling (Oh!, Wow!)

---

## 2️⃣ Subject–Verb Agreement (Most Important)

### Rule 1: Singular subject → Singular verb (+s/es)  
Plural subject → Plural verb (base form)

- He **plays**.  
- They **play**.

---

### Rule 2: “and” joins two subjects → plural

- Ram and Shyam **are** friends.

---

### Rule 3: “Each, Every, Either, Neither, Everyone, Everybody” → singular verb

- Each student **was** present.  
- Neither of them **is** ready.

---

### Rule 4: “One of the + plural noun” → verb singular

- One of the boys **is** absent.

---

### Rule 5: With “as well as / along with / together with”, verb follows first subject

- The manager as well as the staff **is** present.

---

## 3️⃣ Tenses – Key Usage Rules

### Rule 1: Do/Does/Did + V1

- He **does not know**.
- They **do not play**.
- I **did not go**.

Never: did not **went**, does not **knows**.

---

### Rule 2: Has/Have/Had + V3

- He has **gone**.
- They have **finished**.
- She had **written**.

---

### Rule 3: Since / For

- “since” + starting point (since 2010, since Monday, since morning)  
- “for” + duration (for two years, for three days)

- He has been working here **since 2015**.  
- He has been working here **for five years**.

---

## 4️⃣ Articles – a / an / the

### Rule 1: a / an

- “a” before consonant sound: a book, a university (yu)  
- “an” before vowel sound / silent h: an apple, an honest man

---

### Rule 2: “the” used with:

- Unique things: the sun, the moon
- Superlatives: the best, the tallest
- Specific noun: the boy in blue shirt

---

## 5️⃣ Prepositions – Common Exam Rules

- **Discuss** the matter (not discuss about)  
- **Describe** something (not describe about)  
- Good **at** something  
- Interested **in** something  
- Depend **on** someone

Examples:

- They **discussed the plan**.  
- She is good **at** maths.  
- He is interested **in** music.

---

## 6️⃣ Adjectives &amp; Degrees of Comparison

- Positive: tall  
- Comparative: taller than  
- Superlative: the tallest

Rules:

- Never use “**more better**”, “most highest”  
  - Just: better, highest

- After “one of the”, use **plural noun**:
  - one of the **best players**

---

## 7️⃣ Conjunctions – Pair Words

- either … or  
- neither … nor  
- not only … but also  
- both … and

Examples:

- Either you **come** or I **go**.  
- He is **not only** intelligent **but also** hardworking.

---

## 8️⃣ Commonly Tested Confusions

### 8.1 Among vs Between

- **between** – 2 persons/things  
- **among** – more than 2

---

### 8.2 Much vs Many

- **many** – countable (books, students)  
- **much** – uncountable (water, money)

---

### 8.3 Less vs Fewer

- **less** – uncountable (less money)  
- **fewer** – countable (fewer students)

---

### 8.4 Its vs It’s

- **its** – possessive (its colour)  
- **it’s** – it is

---

## 🧪 9. Solved Examples

1. **He do not know** the answer. ❌  
   ✅ He **does not know** the answer.

2. **Each of the players are tired.** ❌  
   ✅ Each of the players **is** tired.

3. **He has finished his work yesterday.** ❌  
   ✅ He **finished** his work yesterday.

4. **She is senior than me.** ❌  
   ✅ She is senior **to** me.

5. **They discussed about the problem.** ❌  
   ✅ They discussed the problem.

6. **One of the boy is absent.** ❌  
   ✅ One of the **boys** is absent.

## 📝 10. Practice Questions (with Answers)

Identify the correct sentence:

```text
1)  A) He do not like tea.
    B) He does not likes tea.
    C) He does not like tea.
    D) He do not likes tea.

2)  A) Each of the girls have a pen.
    B) Each of the girls has a pen.
    C) Each of the girl has a pen.
    D) Each of the girls have pens.

3)  A) She has completed the work yesterday.
    B) She completed the work yesterday.
    C) She has completes the work yesterday.
    D) She has been completed the work yesterday.

4)  A) He is one of the best player in the team.
    B) He is one of the best players in the team.
    C) He is one of best players in the team.
    D) He is one of the better players in the team.

5)  A) They discussed the problem in detail.
    B) They discussed about the problem in detail.
    C) They had discussed about the problem.
    D) They have discussed about the problem.
```

---

### ✅ Answers

1. **C** – does not like  
2. **B** – Each … has  
3. **B** – Simple past with “yesterday”  
4. **B** – one of the best players  
5. **A** – discussed the problem (no “about”)
