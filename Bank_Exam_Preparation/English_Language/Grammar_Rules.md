## 3. Core Areas and Key Rules

### 3.1 Parts of Speech (Quick Map)

1. **Noun** – name of person, place, thing, idea.  
   - student, Mumbai, table, honesty
2. **Pronoun** – stands for a noun.  
   - I, he, she, it, they, who
3. **Verb** – shows action or state.  
   - run, eat, is, seem
4. **Adjective** – describes noun/pronoun.  
   - tall boy, honest person
5. **Adverb** – describes verb, adjective, or another adverb.  
   - runs quickly, very tall
6. **Preposition** – shows relation (place, time, direction).  
   - in, on, at, from, to, with, by
7. **Conjunction** – joins words/phrases/clauses.  
   - and, but, or, because, although
8. **Interjection** – sudden feeling.  
   - Oh! Wow!

---

### 3.2 Subject–Verb Agreement (SVA)

Key rule:  
**Verb must agree with subject in number (singular/plural) and person.**

1. **Basic Present Simple**
   - Singular subject → verb + s/es  
     - He runs. She plays.
   - Plural subject → base verb  
     - They run. We play.

2. **“Each, every, everyone, everybody, neither, either”**
   - Treated as singular.  
     - Each of the players **is** ready.

3. **“A number of / The number of”**
   - A number of + plural noun → verb plural  
     - A number of students **are** missing.
   - The number of + plural noun → verb singular  
     - The number of students **is** increasing.

4. **“As well as, along with, together with”**
   - Verb agrees with first subject.  
     - The manager, as well as his assistants, **was** present.

5. **“Either…or / Neither…nor”**
   - Verb agrees with **nearest subject**.  
     - Neither the teacher nor the students **were** ready.

6. **Uncountable nouns** (news, information, advice, furniture, luggage) → singular.  
   - The news **is** surprising.

---

### 3.3 Tenses – Core Usage

Focus on 3 main tenses for bank exams:

1. **Simple Present**
   - Facts, habits, general truths.  
     - The sun rises in the east.

2. **Present Continuous**
   - Actions happening now / temporary.  
     - She is reading.

3. **Present Perfect**
   - Action finished in past, result seen now.  
     - I have finished my homework.

4. **Simple Past**
   - Completed action at definite time in past.  
     - He visited Delhi last year.

5. **Past Continuous**
   - Action was happening at a particular time in past.  
     - I was watching TV when he called.

6. **Future with “will” / “going to”**
   - Will: instant decisions, promises.  
   - Going to: planned actions.

**Special exam point:**

- “Since” + point in time → Present Perfect / Present Perfect Continuous.  
- “For” + period → duration.

### 3.4 Articles and Determiners

1. **A / An**
   - Used with singular countable nouns:
     - a book, an apple.
   - “An” before vowel sound (a, e, i, o, u) and silent “h”:
     - an hour, an honest man.

2. **The**
   - For specific / known noun:
     - the book on the table.
   - Before unique things: the sun, the earth, the Taj Mahal.
   - Before superlatives: the best, the highest.

3. **No article**
   - With plural/general nouns:  
     - Books are useful.  
   - With uncountable nouns in general sense:  
     - Milk is good for health.

---

### 3.5 Pronouns

1. **Pronoun must match noun in person, number, gender.**

2. **Cases:**
   - Subject: I, he, she, they, we, who  
   - Object: me, him, her, them, us, whom  
   - Possessive: my, his, her, their, our

3. **Reflexive pronouns**: myself, himself, herself, ourselves, themselves.  
   - Used when subject and object are same:
     - He hurt himself.

4. **Common mistake:**  
   - Between you and I → incorrect.  
   - Between you and me → correct (object case after preposition “between”).

---

### 3.6 Prepositions

1. **Place**
   - in – inside something, large area: in a room, in India  
   - on – on surface: on the table  
   - at – specific point: at the door, at 5 pm

2. **Time**
   - in – months/years/long periods: in January, in 2020  
   - on – days/dates: on Monday, on 15th August  
   - at – clock time/festivals: at 5 pm, at Diwali

3. **Common verb + preposition pairs**
   - depend on, insist on, interested in  
   - responsible for, fond of, good at  
   - similar to, aware of, afraid of

---

### 3.7 Conjunctions and Connectors

1. **Coordinating conjunctions** – and, but, or, nor, yet, so.  
   - Join equal parts.

2. **Subordinating conjunctions** – because, although, if, when, while, since.  
   - Join dependent clause to main clause:
     - I left because it was late.

3. **Connector words** (used heavily in RC and Parajumbles)
   - Addition: moreover, also, furthermore
   - Contrast: however, but, on the other hand
   - Result: therefore, thus, hence
   - Example: for example, for instance
   - Conclusion: in conclusion, to sum up

## 4. Level-wise Practice

### 4.1 Level 1 – Core Grammar Checks

**Q1.** Choose the correct sentence.  

A) Each of the players have a new jersey.  
B) Each of the players has a new jersey.  

Answer: **B** – “Each” is singular → has.

---

**Q2.** Choose the correct sentence.  

A) He did not knew the answer.  
B) He did not know the answer.  

Answer: **B** – after “did not”, use base verb “know”.

---

**Q3.** Choose the correct option to fill the blank:  
She has been living in this city ______ 2015.  

A) for  
B) since  

Answer: **B** – since + point in time (2015).

---

### 4.2 Level 2 – Mixed Grammar

**Q4.** Fill in the blank with correct article:  
He is ______ honest man.  

A) a  
B) an  
C) the  
D) no article  

Answer: **B** – “honest” starts with vowel sound /o/; use “an”.

---

**Q5.** Choose the correct pronoun:

This is a secret between you and _____.  

A) I  
B) me  

Answer: **B** – after preposition “between”, use object case “me”.

---

**Q6.** Choose the correct sentence.  

A) The news are very surprising.  
B) The news is very surprising.  

Answer: **B** – “news” is uncountable, singular → is.

---

### 4.3 Level 3 – Exam-level Mixed Errors

Identify the incorrect part.

**Q7.**  
(A) Neither the teacher / (B) nor the students / (C) was interested / (D) in changing the rules.  

- Nearest subject after “nor” = “students” (plural) → verb should be “were”.  
Error in **part C**.

---

**Q8.**  
(A) She has completed / (B) her homework / (C) yesterday / (D) before going out.  

- Present perfect (“has completed”) does not go with definite past time “yesterday”.  
- Correct: She **completed** her homework yesterday…  
Error in **part A** or C combination, but exam style: **C** is often marked due to wrong time word with tense.

---

**Q9.**  
(A) The principal discussed about / (B) the new rules / (C) with the teachers / (D) in the meeting.  

- “Discuss” should not be followed by “about”.  
- Correct: discussed the new rules.  
Error in **part A**.

## 5. Common Traps and Patterns

1. **Did + Verb**
   - Wrong: did not went, did not knew.  
   - Correct: did not go, did not know.

2. **Since vs For**
   - Since + point: since Monday, since 2010.  
   - For + period: for two days, for five years.

3. **Much vs Many / Few vs Little**
   - Many, few → countable nouns (many books, few people).  
   - Much, little → uncountable nouns (much money, little water).

4. **Double Negatives**
   - Wrong: I do not want no help.  
   - Correct: I do not want any help.

5. **Comparisons**
   - senior/junior/superior/inferior → use “to”, not “than”.  
     - He is senior to me.

6. **Pronoun Reference**
   - Pronoun must clearly refer to one noun.
   - Avoid: When the box was near the table, it broke. (What broke?)

## 6. Micro-Revision Summary

For quick grammar revision before exam:

1. **Subject–Verb Agreement**
   - Find subject → match verb number.
   - Watch “each, every, either, neither, as well as, neither/nor”.

2. **Tenses**
   - Simple present/past.
   - Present perfect with since/for.
   - Avoid mixing present perfect with specific past time (yesterday, last year).

3. **Articles and Pronouns**
   - A/An vs The vs no article.
   - I/me, he/him, they/them.

4. **Prepositions**
   - in/on/at (time and place).
   - Common verb + preposition pairs.

5. **Connectors**
   - because (reason), although (contrast), therefore (result).

If you keep these core rules clear and practise regularly, your accuracy in all English topics (Error Spotting, Improvement, Cloze, etc.) will increase sharply.
