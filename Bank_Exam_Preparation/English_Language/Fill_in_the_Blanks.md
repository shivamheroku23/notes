# 🧩 Fill in the Blanks – Context Se Word Choose Karo

> 🧠 Is topic mein ek sentence hota hai, jisme 1 ya 2 blanks hote hain.  
> Aapko **sahi word / phrase** choose karna hota hai.

---

## 🌟 1. Types of Fill in the Blanks

1. **Single blank – vocabulary based**
2. **Double blank – pair of words**
3. **Grammar based** – prepositions, conjunctions, tense
4. **Context based** – meaning dekh ke correct word

---

## 🔑 2. General Strategy (Step-by-Step)

1. **Pehle poora sentence padh ke meaning samjho**.
2. Check:
   - Positive / negative tone?
   - Past / present / future?
   - Contrast (but, however) ya same idea (and, moreover)?
3. Eliminate:
   - Grammatically wrong options
   - Meaning se match na hone wale options
4. Jo bache, unme se **best fit** choose karo.

---

## 📘 3. Grammar-Based Blanks

### 3.1 Preposition

- good **at**
- interested **in**
- prefer tea **to** coffee
- responsible **for**

Example:

> She is good ___ mathematics.  
> (A) in (B) at (C) on (D) for

Correct: **at**

---

### 3.2 Conjunction

- although … **yet/still**
- both … **and**
- either … **or**
- neither … **nor**

Example:

> He is **neither** intelligent ___ hardworking.  
> (A) or (B) and (C) nor (D) but

Correct: **nor**

---

## 📚 4. Vocabulary &amp; Context

Aapko sentence ka mood samajhna hai:

- positive: successful, beneficial, productive…
- negative: harmful, disastrous, risky…

Example:

> Smoking has a ___ effect on health.  
> (A) beneficial (B) harmful (C) productive (D) helpful

Healthy ke context mein, effect negative hoga → **harmful**

---

## 🧠 5. Double Fillers (Two Blanks)

Yahaan **pair of words** sahi ho na chahiye.

Example:

> The teacher was ___ with the student’s performance, so she ___ him.  
> (A) pleased, punished  
> (B) satisfied, rewarded  
> (C) angry, appreciated  
> (D) impressed, ignored

Context: Performance achhi → positive → **satisfied, rewarded**

Correct: **B**

---

## 🧪 6. Solved Examples

### Example 1

> He is ___ honest ___ everyone respects him.  
> (A) so, that  
> (B) too, to  
> (C) very, because  
> (D) more, than

Structure: **so … that**  

Correct: **A**

---

### Example 2

> The old man was too weak ___ walk.  
> (A) to (B) for (C) in (D) on

Idiom: **too … to**  
Correct: **A**

---

### Example 3

> She is not only intelligent ___ also hardworking.  
> (A) and (B) but (C) so (D) or

Pair: not only … **but also**  
Correct: **B**

---

### Example 4

> Due to heavy rains, the match was ___ and the spectators were very ___.  
> (A) continued, happy  
> (B) delayed, angry  
> (C) cancelled, disappointed  
> (D) postponed, delighted

Context negative, spectators disappointed:  
Best pair: **cancelled, disappointed** → **C**

## 📝 7. Practice Questions (with Answers)

```text
1)  She is very fond ___ music.
    A) of   B) in   C) at   D) with

2)  He worked hard ___ he could pass the exam.
    A) because   B) so that   C) although   D) but

3)  The manager was ___ with his performance and decided to ___ him.
    A) pleased, promote
    B) angry, reward
    C) satisfied, punish
    D) happy, remove

4)  Rohan is not only tall ___ also very strong.
    A) and   B) but   C) also   D) yet

5)  The teacher asked the students to ___ the mistakes in their notebooks.
    A) correct   B) do   C) prepare   D) solve

6)  He did not attend the meeting ___ he was unwell.
    A) because   B) although   C) but   D) yet

7)  The government has taken several steps to ___ road accidents.
    A) increase   B) reduce   C) create   D) generate

8)  She was too tired ___ continue the work.
    A) to   B) for   C) of   D) at

9)  Honesty is the best ___.
    A) policy   B) principle   C) rule   D) idea

10) The thief was caught red-handed and ___ to the police.
    A) given   B) handed over   C) left   D) provided
```

---

### ✅ Answers (Brief)

1. fond **of** → A  
2. so that (purpose) → **B**  
3. performance good → **pleased, promote** → A  
4. not only … **but** also → B  
5. correct the mistakes → **A**  
6. did not … because → **A**  
7. reduce accidents → **B**  
8. too tired **to** → A  
9. Honesty is the best **policy** → A  
10. handed over → **B**
