# ✍️ Sentence Improvement – Best Possible Version Choose Karo

> 🧠 Is topic mein ek sentence diya hota hai, aur underlined part ko  
> **improve** karne ke liye options diye jaate hain.

---

## 🌟 1. Question Pattern

Example:

> He **do not knows** how to swim.  
> (A) does not knows  
> (B) does not know  
> (C) do not know  
> (D) no improvement

Correct: **(B) does not know**

Yeh chapter **Error Spotting + Vocabulary + Usage** ka mix hai.

---

## 🔑 2. Kis Type ke Mistakes Aate Hain?

1. **Grammar error**
   - Subject–verb agreement
   - Tense
   - Articles
   - Prepositions
2. **Word choice / idiom**
   - wrong preposition: “discuss about”
   - wrong phrase: “more better”
3. **Redundancy (extra words)**
4. **Formal English usage**
   - prefers tea **to** coffee
   - superior **to**, junior **to**

---

## 🧱 3. Subject–Verb Agreement Recap

- He **does**, They **do**
- She **has**, They **have**
- Each / Every / Neither / Either → singular verb

Example:

> Each of the players **are** ready.  
> Best improvement: **is**

---

## ⏱️ 4. Tense &amp; Verb Form Improvement

- Did + V1  
- Has/Have/Had + V3

Example:

> She did not **went** to school yesterday.  
> Best improvement: did not **go**

---

## 📌 5. Common Patterns with Examples

### 5.1 “More better”, “most highest” – double comparison ❌

> This is the **most highest** building in the city. ❌  
> ✅ This is the **highest** building in the city.

---

### 5.2 Prefer / Senior / Junior / Inferior / Superior – preposition “to”

- prefer tea **to** coffee  
- senior **to** me  
- junior **to** him  

> She prefers tea **than** coffee. ❌  
> ✅ prefers tea **to** coffee.

---

### 5.3 “Despite / In spite of”

- Despite / In spite of + **noun / V+ing**
- Don’t use “despite of”

> Despite of his efforts, he failed. ❌  
> ✅ Despite his efforts, he failed.

---

### 5.4 “Because / Because of”

- because + clause (subject + verb)  
- because of + noun / pronoun

> He failed because of he was careless. ❌  
> ✅ He failed because he was careless.  
> or ✅ He failed because of his carelessness.

---

## 🧠 6. Solved Examples (Step-by-Step)

### Example 1

> He **do not knows** how to drive a car.  
> (A) do not know  
> (B) does not know  
> (C) does not knows  
> (D) no improvement

Explanation:

- Subject = He → **does**
- After does → V1 → “know”

Correct: **does not know** → Option **B**

---

### Example 2

> She is senior **than** me in this office.  
> (A) senior from  
> (B) senior than  
> (C) senior to  
> (D) no improvement

“Senior” takes **to**.

Correct: **senior to me** → Option **C**

---

### Example 3

> He is the **most tallest** boy in the class.  
> (A) taller  
> (B) most tall  
> (C) tallest  
> (D) no improvement

Only one comparison enough → “tallest”

Correct: **tallest** → Option **C**

---

### Example 4

> She insisted **to go** there.  
> (A) on going  
> (B) to going  
> (C) going  
> (D) no improvement

Correct idiom: “insisted **on going**”

Option **A**

---

### Example 5

> In spite of **he was tired**, he finished the work.  
> (A) he being tired  
> (B) his being tired  
> (C) of being tired  
> (D) no improvement

“In spite of / despite” ke baad noun/gerund (V+ing) aata hai.  
Best formal: **his being tired**

Option **B**

## 📝 7. Practice Questions (with Answers)

Choose the best improvement:

```text
1)  He did not went to the market yesterday.
    A) did not gone   B) did not go
    C) not went       D) no improvement

2)  She prefers coffee than tea.
    A) prefers coffee instead tea
    B) prefers coffee more than tea
    C) prefers coffee to tea
    D) no improvement

3)  This is the most fastest train on this route.
    A) the fastest    B) faster
    C) fast           D) no improvement

4)  Inspite of his efforts, he failed in the exam.
    A) In spite of his efforts
    B) Despite of his efforts
    C) In spite his efforts
    D) no improvement

5)  He insisted to meet the manager.
    A) insisted on meet
    B) insisted on meeting
    C) insisted for meeting
    D) no improvement

6)  Each of the boys have a pen.
    A) have pens      B) has a pen
    C) has pens       D) no improvement

7)  She is one of the best player in the team.
    A) best players   B) better player
    C) good player    D) no improvement

8)  They discussed about the problem in detail.
    A) discussed for
    B) discussed on
    C) discussed
    D) no improvement

9)  The climate of Chennai is better than Delhi.
    A) than that of Delhi
    B) than that in Delhi
    C) to Delhi
    D) no improvement

10) He is working here since five years.
    A) from five years
    B) for five years
    C) since the five years
    D) no improvement
```

---

### ✅ Answers (Brief)

1. **B) did not go**  
   Did + V1

2. **C) prefers coffee to tea**  
   Prefer A to B

3. **A) the fastest**  
   No “most + fastest”

4. **A) In spite of his efforts**  
   “In spite of” correct; “Inspite” wrong.

5. **B) insisted on meeting**  
   insisted on + V+ing

6. **B) has a pen**  
   Each → singular

7. **A) best players**  
   “one of the” + plural noun

8. **C) discussed**  
   No “about”

9. **A) than that of Delhi**  
   Climate of Chennai … than (climate) of Delhi.

10. **B) for five years**  
    “for” with period of time; “since” ke saath starting point aata hai.
