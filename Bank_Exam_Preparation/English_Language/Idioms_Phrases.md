# 💬 Idioms &amp; Phrases – Common Exam Favourites

> 🧠 Idiom = fixed phrase jiska **special meaning** hota hai (word-by-word meaning se alag).  
> Bank exams mein direct meaning, RC aur sentence-based questions aate hain.

---

## 🌟 1. Why Idioms Matter in Exams?

- Direct questions:  
  “What is the meaning of ‘hit the nail on the head’?”
- Indirectly in:
  - Sentence improvement
  - RC
  - Cloze test

Isliye kuch **high-frequency idioms** yaad karna bahut useful hai.

---

## 📚 2. Common Idioms with Meanings &amp; Examples

### 2.1 Break the ice

**Meaning:** Start conversation in a tense or awkward situation.

> Example:  
> The teacher told a joke to **break the ice** on the first day of class.

---

### 2.2 Once in a blue moon

**Meaning:** Bahut hi kam, very rarely.

> Example:  
> He goes to the cinema **once in a blue moon**.

---

### 2.3 A piece of cake

**Meaning:** Very easy.

> Example:  
> For her, solving maths problems is **a piece of cake**.

---

### 2.4 Hit the nail on the head

**Meaning:** Bilkul sahi baat kehna; identify the exact problem.

> Example:  
> When he said that lack of planning was the issue, he **hit the nail on the head**.

---

### 2.5 Miss the boat

**Meaning:** Miss an opportunity.

> Example:  
> He **missed the boat** by not applying for the job in time.

---

### 2.6 Burn the midnight oil

**Meaning:** Work late into the night.

> Example:  
> She **burned the midnight oil** to prepare for the exam.

---

### 2.7 Call it a day

**Meaning:** Stop working for the day.

> Example:  
> We are tired now; let’s **call it a day**.

---

### 2.8 In hot water

**Meaning:** In trouble.

> Example:  
> He was in **hot water** after coming late to office many times.

---

### 2.9 Face the music

**Meaning:** Accept the unpleasant results / punishment.

> Example:  
> He cheated in the exam and now he must **face the music**.

---

### 2.10 On cloud nine

**Meaning:** Extremely happy.

> Example:  
> When she got the job, she was **on cloud nine**.

---

## 🧠 3. Memory Tricks

- Make **small stories** using 3–4 idioms.
- Draw funny images in your mind.

Example story:

> You **missed the boat**, so now you are **in hot water**,  
> but your friend **hit the nail on the head** and you both are now **on cloud nine** after getting new jobs.

## 📝 4. Practice Questions (with Answers)

Choose the correct meaning:

```text
1) "A piece of cake"
   A) A big problem
   B) Very easy
   C) Very difficult
   D) Very costly

2) "Once in a blue moon"
   A) Very often
   B) Every day
   C) Very rarely
   D) Twice a week

3) "Burn the midnight oil"
   A) Work late at night
   B) Waste time
   C) Sleep early
   D) Travel at night

4) "Face the music"
   A) Listen to songs
   B) Sing a song
   C) Accept punishment
   D) Play an instrument

5) "Miss the boat"
   A) Miss the journey
   B) Miss the train
   C) Miss the opportunity
   D) Miss the meeting

6) "Break the ice"
   A) Break the glass
   B) Start a conversation
   C) Destroy something
   D) End a friendship

7) "Call it a day"
   A) Start working
   B) Stop working for the day
   C) Go for a holiday
   D) Work overtime

8) "In hot water"
   A) In trouble
   B) In comfort
   C) In a safe place
   D) In a cold place

9) "On cloud nine"
   A) Very sad
   B) Confused
   C) Very happy
   D) Tired

10) "Hit the nail on the head"
    A) Hurt someone
    B) Say exactly the right thing
    C) Make a mistake
    D) Beat someone
```

---

### ✅ Answers

1) B – Very easy  
2) C – Very rarely  
3) A – Work late at night  
4) C – Accept punishment  
5) C – Miss the opportunity  
6) B – Start a conversation  
7) B – Stop working for the day  
8) A – In trouble  
9) C – Very happy  
10) B – Say exactly the right thing
