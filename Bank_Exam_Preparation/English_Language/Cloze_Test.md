# 📚 Cloze Test – Paragraph Mein Blanks Fill Karo

> 🧠 Cloze Test = ek chhota passage + multiple blanks.  
> Har blank ke liye options hote hain. Ye **Reading + Vocabulary + Grammar** ka combo hota hai.

---

## 🌟 1. Strategy for Cloze Test

1. **Pehle pura passage jaldi se read karo** (bina blanks par atke).
2. Passage ka **mood/idea** samjho:
   - Positive? Negative? Problem–solution?
3. Fir blank-by-blank aao:
   - 1–2 lines before and after blank dekho.
   - Eliminate wrong options (grammar/meaning).
4. Options mein se **best fit** lo, not just “possible” fit.

> [!TIP] 💡  
> Kabhi bhi sirf word dekh ke answer mat lagao, hamesha **sentence + passage** dono ka sense dekho.

---

## 🔑 2. Kis type ke words aate hain?

- Tense / verb form (was, is, has been)
- Connectors (however, therefore, because, although)
- Prepositions (in, at, by, for)
- Vocabulary (effect, affect, increase, reduce, etc.)
- Collocations (make a decision, take a step, heavy rain, strong coffee)

---

## 🧪 3. Mini Cloze Test Example (Step-by-Step)

Read the passage and fill the blanks:

> Education is one of the most important (1) ___ in a person’s life. It not only helps us to (2) ___ knowledge but also to develop our character. A well-educated person can (3) ___ better decisions and contribute (4) ___ society. Therefore, governments should (5) ___ more in education.

Options:

1. (A) factors  (B) things  (C) parts  (D) issues  
2. (A) gain     (B) lose    (C) send   (D) divide  
3. (A) do       (B) make    (C) give   (D) take  
4. (A) for      (B) on      (C) to     (D) in  
5. (A) invest   (B) spend   (C) take   (D) earn  

### Solve:

(1) “important ___ in a person’s life”  
Best formal word: **factors** → 1 = A

(2) helps us to __ knowledge  
We “gain knowledge” → 2 = A

(3) can __ better decisions  
Correct collocation: **make decisions** → 3 = B

(4) contribute __ society  
Phrase: contribute **to** society → 4 = C

(5) should __ more in education  
“invest in education” → 5 = A

✅ Answers: 1-A, 2-A, 3-B, 4-C, 5-A

## 🧠 4. Common Connector Words

| Word      | Use / Meaning                       |
|-----------|-------------------------------------|
| however   | contrast (lekin, par)              |
| therefore | result (isliye)                    |
| although  | contrast (yadyapi, halanki)        |
| moreover  | in addition (aur bhi)             |
| instead   | in place of (iske bajaay)          |
| despite   | in spite of (ke bavajud)           |

Example:

> He worked very hard; **however**, he could not pass.  
> He was ill; **therefore**, he stayed at home.

## 📝 5. Practice Cloze Test (with Answers)

### Passage:

> In today’s world, the Internet has become an essential part of our daily life. It is used to (1) ___ information, communicate with others and even do shopping. However, excessive use of the Internet can (2) ___ many problems. People may become (3) ___ and ignore their studies or work. Therefore, it is important to (4) ___ a balance between online and offline activities. Parents should also (5) ___ their children and guide them to use the Internet wisely.

Options:

1. (A) gain  (B) obtain  (C) collect  (D) All of these  
2. (A) create  (B) cause  (C) bring  (D) show  
3. (A) active  (B) addicted  (C) social  (D) interested  
4. (A) keep  (B) do  (C) make  (D) take  
5. (A) ignore  (B) leave  (C) monitor  (D) avoid  

---

### ✅ Suggested Answers &amp; Explanation

1. “used to ___ information”  
   All: gain/obtain/collect information are possible, but option (D) = All of these is **not fitting grammatically** as a single verb.  
   Best: **collect** information → (C)  
   (Many exams also use “obtain information” → but we choose the **most natural**.)

2. “can ___ many problems”  
   We usually say “cause problems” → (B)  

3. “become ___ and ignore their studies” → negative  
   “addicted” is perfect → (B)

4. “important to ___ a balance”  
   Phrase: “keep a balance” or “maintain a balance”; among options, best = **keep** → (A)

5. “Parents should also ___ their children”  
   Obviously not ignore/leave/avoid → **monitor their children** → (C)

✅ Final: 1-C, 2-B, 3-B, 4-A, 5-C
