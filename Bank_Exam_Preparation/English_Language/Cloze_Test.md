# Cloze Test – Advanced Bible

---

## 1. Concept Map

- **Goal**: Fill multiple blanks in a passage so that the **whole story makes sense**.
- Tested skills:
  - Reading comprehension
  - Vocabulary and collocations
  - Grammar (prepositions, articles, tenses, connectors)
  - Logical flow of ideas

Cloze Test is like **Fill in the Blanks + Reading Comprehension** combined.

---

## 2. Deep Logic – How to Think

### 2.1 Core Idea

In Cloze Test, you are not just filling single sentences.  
You are building a **connected paragraph**.

So you must:

1. Understand the **main theme** of the passage.
2. Track **tone and direction** (problem, solution, example, conclusion).
3. Use both:
   - **Local clues** (words just before and after the blank)
   - **Global clues** (what is happening in the whole passage)

### 2.2 Reading Strategy

There are two workable strategies:

1. **Sentence-by-sentence with backward check**
   - Read first sentence, fill its blank(s).
   - Go to next, but keep checking if earlier choices still fit.

2. **Full reading first, then blanks**
   - Read the whole passage quickly once.
   - Then solve blanks with clarity of theme and tone.

For beginners, method 1 is easier; for advanced speed, method 2 is better.

---

## 3. Algorithm / Template

### 3.1 Cloze Test Solving Algorithm

**Step 1 – Skim the Passage**

- Read quickly to understand:
  - Topic
  - Basic storyline
  - Whether it is positive/negative, factual/opinion, problem/solution etc.

**Step 2 – Start with First Blank**

- Look at words just before and after blank.
- Identify required part of speech:
  - Noun / verb / adjective / adverb / preposition / connector.

**Step 3 – Use Option Elimination**

- Remove options that:
  - Do not fit grammatically.
  - Break the tone or logic.
  - Do not match the theme.

**Step 4 – Check Logical Flow**

- After filling a blank, read the sentence again.
- Ask: Does it connect logically with the previous and next sentences?

**Step 5 – Maintain Consistency**

- Tense should remain consistent (unless clear change in time).
- Tone and point of view should stay stable.

**Step 6 – Final Review**

- After filling all blanks, read the entire passage once.
- If any sentence sounds odd or forced, re-check that blank.

## 4. Level-wise Practice

### 4.1 Level 1 – Simple Cloze (Concept Demo)

Read the passage and choose the correct options.

Passage:

Today, most people are ______(1) by a fast-paced lifestyle. They have little time to relax or to spend with their families. As a result, they often feel stressed and ______(2). One simple way to deal with this problem is to develop the habit of regular exercise. Even a short walk every day can have a ______(3) effect on both the body and mind. Exercise helps to release chemicals in the brain that improve our mood and make us feel more ______(4). Therefore, health experts ______(5) people to include some physical activity in their daily routine.

Options:

1)  
A) enjoying  
B) living  
C) busy  
D) affected  

2)  
A) energetic  
B) relaxed  
C) exhausted  
D) happy  

3)  
A) harmful  
B) negative  
C) powerful  
D) vague  

4)  
A) anxious  
B) depressed  
C) cheerful  
D) dull  

5)  
A) suggest  
B) force  
C) ignore  
D) prevent  

---

**Solution L1 – Step-by-step**

(1) “Today, most people are ______ by a fast-paced lifestyle.”  
- Structure: are + past participle (usually).  
- “affected by a fast-paced lifestyle” is a common phrase.  
- Answer: **D) affected**.

(2) “they often feel stressed and ______.”  
- If stressed, likely negative second word.  
- “energetic” and “relaxed” are positive.  
- “exhausted” fits: “stressed and exhausted.”  
- Answer: **C) exhausted**.

(3) “can have a ______ effect on both the body and mind.”  
- Effect should be positive, strong.  
- “powerful effect” is standard collocation.  
- Answer: **C) powerful**.

(4) “make us feel more ______.”  
- After exercise, feeling is positive.  
- Options: anxious, depressed, cheerful, dull.  
- “cheerful” is correct.  
- Answer: **C) cheerful**.

(5) “health experts ______ people to include some physical activity…”  
- They “suggest” / “advise” people.  
- “suggest” is best.  
- Answer: **A) suggest**.

### 4.2 Level 2 – Grammar + Collocation

Passage:

Books have always played an important ______(1) in shaping human civilization. They not only provide knowledge but also ______(2) our imagination and creativity. In the digital age, many people believe that printed books will soon ______(3) by e-books. However, the experience of holding a book in hand and turning its pages is still ______(4) by millions of readers around the world. Therefore, it is unlikely that printed books will ______(5) disappear.

Options:

1)  
A) position  
B) role  
C) character  
D) duty  

2)  
A) inspire  
B) destroy  
C) copy  
D) reduce  

3)  
A) replace  
B) be replacing  
C) be replaced  
D) replaced  

4)  
A) enjoyed  
B) enjoying  
C) enjoy  
D) enjoyment  

5)  
A) rarely  
B) completely  
C) partly  
D) suddenly  

---

**Solution L2 – Step-by-step**

(1) “played an important ______ in shaping human civilization.”  
- Phrase: “play an important role” is standard.  
- Answer: **B) role**.

(2) “provide knowledge but also ______ our imagination.”  
- Provide knowledge and also **inspire** imagination.  
- Answer: **A) inspire**.

(3) “will soon ______ by e-books.”  
- Passive structure: will soon **be replaced** by.  
- Answer: **C) be replaced**.

(4) “experience … is still ______ by millions of readers.”  
- Experience enjoyed by → passive: “is still enjoyed by”.  
- Answer: **A) enjoyed**.

(5) “unlikely that printed books will ______ disappear.”  
- Phrase: “completely disappear” is standard.  
- Answer: **B) completely**.

### 4.3 Level 3 – Exam-level Cloze

Passage:

Effective communication is one of the most important skills in the workplace. Yet, many employees fail to ______(1) their ideas clearly. Sometimes, they use very complex language in an attempt to sound professional, which only ______(2) the listener. At other times, they do not listen carefully and ______(3) important details shared by others. To improve communication, it is essential to speak in a simple and clear manner, and to ______(4) attention to what others are saying. Good communication not only helps in avoiding misunderstandings but also ______(5) strong relationships among team members.

Options:

1)  
A) hide  
B) express  
C) impress  
D) control  

2)  
A) encourages  
B) confuses  
C) motivates  
D) admires  

3)  
A) notice  
B) understand  
C) miss  
D) record  

4)  
A) pay  
B) give  
C) take  
D) bring  

5)  
A) weakens  
B) develops  
C) destroys  
D) ignores  

---

**Solution L3 – Step-by-step**

(1) “fail to ______ their ideas clearly.”  
- We “express ideas”.  
- Answer: **B) express**.

(2) “which only ______ the listener.”  
- Complex language usually confuses.  
- Answer: **B) confuses**.

(3) “do not listen carefully and ______ important details.”  
- If not listening, you “miss details”.  
- Answer: **C) miss**.

(4) “and to ______ attention to what others are saying.”  
- Standard phrase: “pay attention”.  
- Answer: **A) pay**.

(5) “helps in avoiding misunderstandings but also ______ strong relationships…”  
- Good communication builds / develops strong relationships.  
- Best option: **B) develops**.

## 5. Common Traps and How to Avoid Them

1. **Looking Only at Local Meaning**
   - If you see each blank in isolation, you may choose wrong words.
   - Always check if your choice fits the **overall story**.

2. **Ignoring Collocations**
   - Many blanks test common word pairs:
     - heavy rain, strong possibility, pay attention, take part, make a decision.
   - Learn such pairs; they give quick clues.

3. **Not Noticing Connectors**
   - Words like although, however, therefore, but, so, yet, despite tell you whether:
     - Next sentence supports, contrasts or concludes previous idea.
   - Choose words that maintain that relation.

4. **Tense and Pronoun Inconsistency**
   - Check that tense remains consistent across the passage.
   - Pronouns (he, she, they, it) should refer clearly to some noun.

5. **Overthinking Rare Words**
   - In most bank exams, answers use **common, natural words**.
   - Rare, difficult words are often traps.

---

## 6. Micro-Revision Summary

Quick checklist before solving Cloze Tests:

1. **Skim first** – know topic and tone.
2. **For each blank**:
   - Check part of speech.
   - Check local grammar.
   - Use meaning + collocation.
3. **Use connectors and theme** as global guides.
4. **After filling all blanks**, read passage once to ensure smooth flow.
5. Prefer:
   - Natural collocations
   - Consistent tense and tone
   - Clear, simple expression

Practise regularly using this structured approach and you will find Cloze Tests becoming **predictable and scoring** in bank exams.
