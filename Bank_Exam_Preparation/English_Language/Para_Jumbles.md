# 🔀 Para Jumbles – Sentences Ko Sahi Order Mein Lagao

> 🧠 Para jumbles mein aapko 4–6 sentences diye jaate hain (A, B, C, D, E, F)  
> Aur aapko **correct sequence** banana hota hai jisse **meaningful paragraph** ban jaaye.

---

## 🌟 1. Basic Strategy – Very Simple

1. **Pehle overall topic samjho**  
   - Sab sentences ek hi topic par hote hain.
2. **Opening sentence (intro) dhundo**  
   - Generally:
     - Naya idea introduce karta hai.
     - “This, these, such” se start nahi hota.
3. **Mandatory pairs** dhundo  
   - Jaise: A ke baad hamesha B jaisa feel aaye.
4. **Pronoun + Noun linking**  
   - “Rahul …” ke baad “He …”
   - “these problems” ke liye pehle problems ka zikr hoga.
5. **Connectors &amp; sequence words**  
   - however, therefore, moreover, first, secondly, finally

> [!TIP] 💡  
> Pehle rough order banao, phir options se **match** karo.  

---

## 🧩 2. Opening &amp; Closing Sentence Clues

### Opening Sentence:

- General statement
- No “this/that/these/those/such problem”
- No pronoun without reference (he, she, they, it)

### Closing Sentence:

- Often summary, advice, result
- Words like: therefore, thus, hence, in conclusion

---

## 🧪 3. Simple Example (4 Sentences)

Sentences:

A. Exercise keeps our body fit and healthy.  
B. Therefore, everyone should make it a part of their daily routine.  
C. It also helps us to reduce stress and improve mood.  
D. In today’s busy life, most people do not find time to exercise.

---

### Step-by-Step

1. Topic: Exercise  
2. Which looks like intro?

   - A: Exercise keeps our body fit and healthy. (Intro-like)  
   - D: In today’s busy life… (also intro-type, context of problem)

   Common pattern:  
   - Pehle problem (D), phir benefits (A, C), phir advice (B).

3. Now pair making:

   - A: “Exercise keeps our body fit and healthy.”  
   - C: “It also helps us to reduce stress…” → “It” = Exercise  
     So A–C is a clear pair.

4. B starts with “Therefore” → closing sentence.

So sensible order:

D (problem) → A (benefit 1) → C (benefit 2) → B (advice)

✅ Answer: **D A C B**

---

## 🧠 4. Useful Clue Types

- **Name → Pronoun**  
  - “Rahul…” then “He…”
- **Singular → These / Such / This**  
  - First mention problem, then: “This problem…”
- **Sequence words**  
  - Firstly, secondly, finally
- **Cause–Effect**  
  - cause: because, due to, since  
  - effect: therefore, as a result, hence

## 📝 5. Practice Para Jumbles (with Answers)

### Question 1

Arrange the sentences to form a meaningful paragraph:

A. It helps us to stay updated with news from all over the world.  
B. The newspaper is a very useful source of information.  
C. Therefore, reading a newspaper daily is a good habit.  
D. It also contains articles on sports, business, entertainment and many other topics.

---

Think:

- Intro? → B (defines newspaper)  
- It? → A/D (It = newspaper)  
- Closing advice? → C (“Therefore…”)

Now pair:

- B → (It helps us…) → A  
- It also contains… → D  
- Finally → C

We can do:

B (intro) → A (helps us stay updated) → D (also contains articles) → C (Therefore…)

✅ Order: **B A D C**

---

### Question 2

Sentences:

A. Water is essential for the survival of all living beings.  
B. In many parts of the world, people do not have access to clean drinking water.  
C. Governments and organizations are working to provide safe water to everyone.  
D. Without water, life on Earth would not be possible.  

Find correct order.

Clues:

- Intro: general statement about water? A or D.  
  A: “Water is essential…”  
  D: “Without water, life on Earth…” – seems like further explanation.

So: A → D (support)

Then: B (problem) and C (solution).

So order:

A (intro) → D (importance) → B (problem) → C (solution)

✅ **A D B C**

---

## ✅ Answers Summary

- Q1 → **B A D C**
- Q2 → **A D B C**

> 2) 2–3 strong pairs banao (A–C, B–D type)  
> 3) Fir options se match karo  
> Isse tumhara time kaafi bach jayega aur accuracy bhi high hogi.
