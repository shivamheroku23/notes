# 🧠 Vocabulary – Synonyms &amp; Antonyms (Bank Exam Focus)

> 🧠 Synonym = same / similar meaning word  
> Antonym = opposite meaning word  

Bank exams mein:

- Direct synonym/antonym questions,
- RC, Cloze, Error, Sentence Improvement – sab mein vocab ka role hota hai.

---

## 🌟 1. Synonyms &amp; Antonyms – Basic Idea

- **Synonym**:  
  - small – little  
  - big – large  
  - happy – glad

- **Antonym**:  
  - big – small  
  - happy – sad  
  - increase – decrease

## 📘 3. Example Questions – Synonyms

**Q1.** Choose the word which is **most similar** in meaning to “ENORMOUS”:

A) tiny  
B) huge  
C) narrow  
D) thin  

✅ Answer: **B) huge**

---

**Q2.** Choose the synonym of “FRAGILE”:

A) strong  
B) weak  
C) permanent  
D) careful  

✅ Answer: **B) weak**

## 📕 4. Example Questions – Antonyms

**Q3.** Choose the word which is **opposite** in meaning to “BENEFICIAL”:

A) useful  
B) harmful  
C) helpful  
D) healthy  

✅ Answer: **B) harmful**

---

**Q4.** Antonym of “ACCEPT”:

A) admit  
B) agree

✅ Answer: **C) refuse**

## 🧠 5. Memory Techniques

- **Word Roots**:  
  - “micro” = small (microscope)  
  - “tele” = distance (telephone, television)  
  - “auto” = self (autograph)

- **Visual Method**:  
  Word + image imagine karo.  
  - Enormous = imagine a **very big elephant**.  
  - Fragile = imagine **glass** breaking easily.

- **Story Method**:  
  Chhoti story banao:  
  > A **genuine** friend will never **abandon** you in **adverse** conditions.

Isse teen words ek saath yaad rahenge.

## 📝 6. Practice Set – Synonyms &amp; Antonyms

Choose the correct option:

```text
1) Synonym of "GENUINE":
   A) fake   B) real   C) cheap   D) easy

2) Antonym of "DENY":
   A) refuse   B) reject   C) accept   D) ignore

3) Synonym of "OBSCURE":
   A) clear   B) hidden   C) bright   D) simple

4) Antonym of "FRAGILE":
   A) weak   B) strong   C) soft   D) light

5) Synonym of "ADVERSE":
   A) harmful   B) lucky   C) similar   D) useful

6) Antonym of "ABANDON":
   A) leave   B) ignore   C) continue   D) neglect

7) Synonym of "ACCUMULATE":
   A) gather   B) reduce   C) spend   D) lose

8) Antonym of "ENORMOUS":
   A) huge   B) large   C) small   D) wide

9) Synonym of "MAINTAIN":
   A) break   B) preserve   C) destroy   D) disturb

10) Antonym of "ADEQUATE":
    A) sufficient   B) enough   C) large   D) insufficient
```

---

### ✅ Answers

1. **B) real**  
2. **C) accept**  
3. **B) hidden**  
4. **B) strong**  
5. **A) harmful**  
6. **C) continue**  
7. **A) gather**  
8. **C) small**  
9. **B) preserve**  
10. **D) insufficient**
