# 🧪 Mixture &amp; Alligation – Fast Ratio Method

> 🧠 Ye chapter **ratio + average** ka mix hai.  
> Alligation method se mixture wale questions bahut fast ho jate hain.

---

## 🌟 1. Basic Mixture Idea

2 cheezen mila ke:

- New mixture ka **average value** (price, concentration, percentage) nikalna  
- Ya given average se **ratio** nikalna

---

## 🧮 2. Alligation Rule (Simple)

When two types of items with values:

- Cheaper (C)
- Dearer (D)

Mixed to get mean (average) M:

```text
           D − M      :     M − C
Ratio =  -----------------------------
              C part       :   D part
```

> [!TIP] 💡  
> Difference **cross** mein likho:
>
> C —— M —— D  
>      ↖   ↗  
>   D−M   M−C

---

## 📘 3. Example – Price Mixture

Tea at ₹20/kg and ₹30/kg are mixed to get mixture of ₹26/kg. Find ratio of cheaper to dearer tea.

Here:

- C = 20  
- D = 30  
- M = 26

Apply alligation:

```text
        D − M       :      M − C
       30 − 26      :     26 − 20
      = 4           :     6
      = 2           :     3
```

So **C : D = 2 : 3**

Cheaper : Dearer = 2 : 3

---

## 💧 4. Example – Milk &amp; Water (Percentage)

Solution A has 30% milk, B has 50% milk. In what ratio should they be mixed to get 40% milk?

C = 30, D = 50, M = 40

```text
D − M : M − C
50−40 : 40−30 = 10 : 10 = 1 : 1
```

So mix in ratio **1 : 1**.

---

## 🧪 5. Removal &amp; Replacement (Simple Concept)

If from a mixture, some quantity removed and same quantity added of another liquid, repeated operations → advanced; bank prelims me basic level hi aata hai.

Simple case:

> A container has 40 litres of milk. 8 litres milk removed and replaced by water. What is quantity of milk now?

Milk left after removal:

= 40 − 8 = 32 litres  
Water added = 8 litres

Total = 40 litres, with 32 litres milk, 8 litres water.

## 📝 6. Practice Questions (with Answers)

```text
1)  Two types of rice costing ₹25/kg and ₹35/kg are mixed to get mixture worth ₹31/kg.
    Find the ratio of cheaper to dearer rice.

2)  A shopkeeper mixes tea costing ₹100/kg and ₹160/kg and sells the mixture at ₹140/kg
    without profit or loss. Find the ratio of two types of tea.

3)  A solution has 20% alcohol and another has 50% alcohol.
    In what ratio should they be mixed to obtain a 40% alcohol solution?

4)  In what ratio should milk costing ₹30/litre be mixed with milk costing ₹40/litre
    so that the mixture costs ₹36/litre?

5)  A container has 60 litres of milk. 12 litres of milk is taken out and replaced with water.
    How much milk is there now in the container?
```

---

### ✅ Answers (Brief)

1) C=25, D=35, M=31

```text
D−M : M−C = 35−31 : 31−25 = 4 : 6 = 2 : 3
Cheaper : Dearer = 2 : 3
```

2) C=100, D=160, M=140

```text
D−M : M−C = 160−140 : 140−100 = 20 : 40 = 1 : 2
Cheaper : Dearer = 1 : 2
```

3) C=20, D=50, M=40

```text
D−M : M−C = 50−40 : 40−20 = 10 : 20 = 1 : 2
Solution with 20% : 50% = 1 : 2
```

4) C=30, D=40, M=36

```text
D−M : M−C = 40−36 : 36−30 = 4 : 6 = 2 : 3
So 30₹ milk : 40₹ milk = 2 : 3
```

5) 60 litres milk → remove 12 → left = 48 litres milk  
   Add 12 litres water → total 60 litres, milk = **48 litres**
