# 🚰 Pipes &amp; Cisterns – Time &amp; Work in Water Form

> 🧠 Pipes &amp; Cisterns = Time &amp; Work ka hi special case hai.  
> Agar Time &amp; Work ka “1-day work / total work” clear hai, to ye chapter automatically easy ban jata hai.

---

## 🧭 Concept Map – Kya Test Karta Hai?

- Fill / empty problems = **work done per hour**
- Combination of filling &amp; emptying pipes
- Fraction of tank filled / emptied in given time
- Mains me kabhi-kabhi pattern-based questions (alternate opening, leak, etc.)

---

## 🌟 1. Core Idea – 1 Hour’s Work

Exactly Time &amp; Work logic:

- If a pipe fills a tank in T hours:

```text
Filling pipe’s 1-hour work = +1/T  (fraction of tank)
```

- If it empties in E hours:

```text
Emptying pipe’s 1-hour work = −1/E
```

- When both open together:

```text
Net 1-hour work = sum of works (fill − empty)
Time to fill tank = 1 / (net 1-hour filling work)
```

---

## 🧮 2. Standard Patterns &amp; Templates

### 🔹 Template A – Two Filling Pipes

If A fills in a hours, B fills in b hours:

- A’s 1-hr work = 1/a  
- B’s 1-hr work = 1/b  
- Combined 1-hr work = 1/a + 1/b  
- Time = 1 ÷ (1/a+1/b) = ab/(a+b)

Same formula as Time &amp; Work (A+B).

---

#### Example 1 – Two Filling Pipes (L1)

Pipe A → 10 hours, Pipe B → 15 hours. Together?

```text
Time = (10×15)/(10+15) = 150/25 = 6 hours
```

---

### 🔹 Template B – One Filling, One Emptying

If A fills in a hours, B empties same tank in b hours:

1-hr works:

- A: +1/a  
- B: −1/b

Net work per hour:

```text
= 1/a − 1/b
```

If net work positive → tank fills in:

```text
Time = 1 / (1/a − 1/b)
```

If net negative → mixture empties (never fills if starting empty).

---

#### Example 2 – Fill &amp; Empty Together (L1)

A fills in 12 h, B empties in 18 h. Both open, time to fill?

A: +1/12, B: −1/18

```text
Net = 1/12 − 1/18 = (3 − 2)/36 = 1/36
→ 1 hour me 1/36 tank
→ Full tank = 36 hours
```

---

### 🔹 Template C – Stronger Emptying Pipe

If emptying pipe ka magnitude zyada ho → net work −ve.

Example:

A fills in 10 h → +1/10  
B empties in 5 h → −1/5 = −2/10

Net = 1/10 − 2/10 = −1/10

→ 1 hour me 1/10 tank **bhej diya ja raha** (agar full hota to 10 ghante me empty).

---

### 🔹 Template D – Fraction of Tank / Initial Level

Type:

> Tank is 3/4 full, pipe fills in 12 h when empty. In how much time will it become full?

Total work = 1 tank; 1-hr work of pipe = 1/12.

3/4 full → remaining work = 1/4.

Time:

```text
Time = (Remaining work) / (work per hour)
     = (1/4) ÷ (1/12)
     = (1/4)×12
     = 3 hours
```

## 🎯 Levels of Questions

### LEVEL 1 – Direct Formula-Based

1) Pipe A fills in 8 h, B fills in 12 h. Together?

A:1/8, B:1/12 → net= (3+2)/24=5/24

Time = 24/5 = **4.8 h**

---

2) A fills 10 h, B empties 15 h. Both open, time to fill?

Net = 1/10 − 1/15 = (3−2)/30 = 1/30 → 30 h

---

### LEVEL 2 – Slightly Twisted

3) A fills in 15 h, B in 20 h, C empties in 30 h. All open, time to fill?

Works:

- A: 1/15  
- B: 1/20  
- C: −1/30  

LCM = 60:

```text
1/15 = 4/60
1/20 = 3/60
−1/30 = −2/60

Net = (4+3−2)/60 = 5/60 = 1/12
```

Time = **12 h**

---

4) Tank is 1/3 full. Pipe can fill whole tank in 9 hours. In how many hours will tank be completely full?

Remaining work = 2/3  
1-hr work = 1/9

Time = (2/3) ÷ (1/9) = (2/3)×9 = 6 h

---

### LEVEL 3 – Mains Pattern (Longer Story)

5) Pipe A alone can fill a tank in 12 hours. Pipe B alone can fill it in 18 hours. A and B are opened together, but after 3 hours B is closed. In how many more hours will the tank be full?

A: 1/12, B: 1/18

First 3 hours:

```text
Combined 1-hr work = 1/12 + 1/18 = (3+2)/36 = 5/36
Work in 3 hours = 3×5/36 = 15/36 = 5/12
```

Remaining work = 1 − 5/12 = 7/12

Now only A:

```text
A’s 1-hr work = 1/12
Time = (7/12) ÷ (1/12) = 7 hours
```

Total time = 3 + 7 = **10 hours**

## ⚠️ Common Traps &amp; Mistakes

1. **Sign confusion**  
   - Filling = positive, emptying = negative.

2. **Assuming tank always empty at start**  
   - Question me “half / 3/4 full / empty” likha ho → us fraction se start karo.

3. **Forgetting fraction of tank**  
   - “2/3 of tank filled in X hours” type me total time nikalne ke liye direct proportion use karo.

---

## 🧾 Micro-Revision Box

- Fill in T hours → 1-hr work = +1/T  
- Empty in E hours → 1-hr work = −1/E  
- Net 1-hr work = sum of all  
- Time to fill from empty:

```text
Time = 1 / (net +ve work per hour)
```

- Useful patterns:

  1. Two filling pipes → ab/(a+b)  
  2. Fill + empty → 1/(1/a − 1/b) (if result +ve)  
  3. Start partially filled → Remaining work ÷ net 1-hr work
