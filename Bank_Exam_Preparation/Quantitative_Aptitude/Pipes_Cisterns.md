# 🚰 Pipes &amp; Cisterns – Time &amp; Work with Water Tanks

> 🧠 Ye chapter bilkul Time &amp; Work jaisa hai, bas “work” ki jagah “tank fill/empty” hai.

---

## 🌟 1. Basic Idea

If a pipe fills a tank in **T hours**:

- 1 hour ka kaam (fill) = 1/T (part of tank)

If another pipe empties in **E hours**:

- 1 hour ka kaam (empty) = 1/E (part of tank out)

Net effect when both open:

```text
Net 1-hour work = Filling − Emptying
```

---

## 🧮 2. Solved Examples

### Example 1 – Two Filling Pipes

Pipe A can fill a tank in 10 hours, Pipe B in 15 hours. If both opened together, time taken to fill tank?

A’s 1-hour work = 1/10  
B’s 1-hour work = 1/15  

Together:

```text
= 1/10 + 1/15
= (3 + 2)/30
= 5/30 = 1/6
```

So, time = 6 hours.

✅ Answer: **6 hours**

---

### Example 2 – One Filling, One Emptying

Pipe A can fill a tank in 12 hours, Pipe B can empty it in 18 hours. If both opened together, time taken to fill tank?

A’s 1-hour work = +1/12  
B’s 1-hour work = −1/18  

Net:

```text
= 1/12 − 1/18
= (3 − 2)/36
= 1/36
```

So, in 1 hour 1/36 tank fills → full tank = **36 hours**

---

### Example 3 – Emptying Pipe Stronger

Pipe A can fill in 10 hours, Pipe B empties in 5 hours. Both opened → net?

A: +1/10, B: −1/5 = −2/10

Net per hour:

```text
= 1/10 − 2/10 = −1/10
```

Tank will **never fill**; instead, it empties in 10 hours if full.

---

## 🧠 3. Memory Trick

- Treat “fill” as **positive work**.
- Treat “empty” as **negative work**.
- Use same method as Time &amp; Work → 1-hour work +/−.

## 📝 4. Practice Questions (with Answers)

```text
1)  Pipe A can fill a tank in 8 hours and Pipe B in 12 hours.
    How long will they take together to fill the tank?

2)  Pipe A can fill a tank in 15 hours, B can fill it in 20 hours and C can empty it in 30 hours.
    If all are opened together, in how much time will the tank be filled?

3)  A tap can fill a tank in 10 hours. Another tap can empty the tank in 15 hours.
    If both taps are opened together, how long will it take to fill the tank?

4)  A tank is 3/4 full. Pipe A can fill it in 12 hours. In how many hours will it become full?

5)  Two pipes can fill a tank in 12 and 18 hours respectively.
    If both are opened together, in how much time will 2/3 of the tank be filled?
```

---

### ✅ Answers (Brief)

1) A: 1/8, B: 1/12

```text
Total = 1/8 + 1/12 = (3 + 2)/24 = 5/24
Time = 24/5 = 4.8 hours
```

2) A: 1/15, B: 1/20, C: −1/30

```text
Total = 1/15 + 1/20 − 1/30
LCM = 60 → (4 + 3 − 2)/60 = 5/60 = 1/12
Time = 12 hours
```

3) A: 1/10, B: −1/15

```text
Net = 1/10 − 1/15 = (3 − 2)/30 = 1/30
Time = 30 hours
```

4) 3/4 full → remaining = 1/4  
   A fills full in 12 hours → 1-hour work = 1/12  

Time for 1/4:

```text
= (1/4) ÷ (1/12) = (1/4)×12 = 3 hours
```

5) Together 1-hour work:

```text
= 1/12 + 1/18
= (3 + 2)/36 = 5/36
```

To fill 2/3:

```text
Time = (2/3) ÷ (5/36) = (2/3)×(36/5) = 24/5 = 4.8 hours
```
