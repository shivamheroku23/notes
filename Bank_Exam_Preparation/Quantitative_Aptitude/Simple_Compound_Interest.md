# 💵 Simple Interest &amp; Compound Interest – Fast Banking Style

> 🧠 Bank exams mein SI–CI bahut common hai, specially word problems &amp; DI.

---

## 🌟 1. Simple Interest (SI)

**Simple Interest**: Interest only on **principal** (original amount), har saal same.

### Formula

```text
SI = (P × R × T) / 100

Amount A = P + SI
```

Where:

- `P` = Principal ( मूलधन )
- `R` = Rate % per annum
- `T` = Time in years

---

### Example 1 – Basic SI

Find SI on ₹5000 at 10% p.a. for 3 years.

```text
SI = (P × R × T)/100
   = (5000 × 10 × 3)/100
   = 5000 × 30 / 100
   = 150000 / 100
   = 1500
```

Amount A = P + SI = 5000 + 1500 = **₹6500**

---

### Example 2 – Find Rate

SI = ₹900, P = ₹3000, T = 3 years. Find R.

```text
SI = (P×R×T)/100
900 = 3000×R×3 /100
900 = 9000R /100
900 = 90R
R = 900/90 = 10% p.a.
```

---

## 🧮 2. Compound Interest (CI)

**Compound Interest**: Interest added to principal after each period, next year interest on **(P + previous interest)**.

### Annual Compounding Formula

```text
A = P (1 + R/100)^T
CI = A − P
```

---

### Example 3 – Basic CI

Find CI on ₹10,000 at 10% p.a. compounded annually for 2 years.

Amount:

```text
A = 10000 (1 + 10/100)^2
  = 10000 (1.1)^2
  = 10000 × 1.21
  = 12100
CI = A − P = 12100 − 10000 = 2100
```

✅ CI = **₹2100**, Amount = **₹12,100**

---

### Concept Shortcut (Manual Year-wise)

Same question:

Year 1:
- 10% of 10000 = 1000 → new amount = 11000

Year 2:
- 10% of 11000 = 1100 → new amount = 12100

Total CI = 1000 + 1100 = **2100**

> [!TIP] 💡  
> 2–3 saal wale CI questions often easy with **year-wise** method, no need to use full formula.

---

## 🔁 3. Difference between CI &amp; SI (2–3 Years)

### For 2 Years (Same P, R, T)

```text
Difference (CI − SI) for 2 years = P × (R/100)^2
```

### Example 4

P = 8000, R = 10%, T = 2 years. Find CI − SI.

```text
Difference = 8000 × (10/100)^2
           = 8000 × (1/10)^2
           = 8000 × 1/100
           = 80
```

✅ CI is ₹80 more than SI.

(If SI found, add 80 to get CI.)

---

### For 3 Years

```text
Difference (CI − SI) for 3 years = P × (R/100)^2 × (3 + R/100)
```

(Mains level, used rarely but helpful.)

---

## 🧠 4. Memory / Speed Tricks

- **“PRT/100”** – Simple Interest = PRT/100
- CI for **2 years** →  
  - Use `P(1+R/100)^2` or  
  - Do year-wise interest:
    - Year1: P×R/100
    - Year2: (P+Year1 interest)×R/100
- Difference 2 years → **P(R/100)²**

---

## 📘 5. Solved Examples

### Example 5 – Find Time

SI on ₹5000 at 8% p.a. is ₹2000. For how many years?

SI = PRT/100

```text
2000 = 5000 × 8 × T /100
2000 = 400T
T = 2000/400 = 5 years
```

✅ T = **5 years**

---

### Example 6 – CI with Fraction Rate

Find amount on ₹8000 at 12.5% p.a. for 2 years, compounded annually.

12.5% = 1/8

So factor:

```text
(1 + 1/8) = 9/8
A = 8000 × (9/8)^2
  = 8000 × (81/64)
  = 8000/64 × 81
  = 125 × 81
  = 10125
CI = 10125 – 8000 = 2125
```

✅ Amount = **₹10,125**, CI = **₹2125**

---

### Example 7 – CI with Different Time (1.5 years, half-yearly)

This is mains level; here just logic:

- If 10% p.a. compounded **half-yearly**, then:
  - Each half-year rate = 10/2 = 5%
  - Time 1.5 years = 3 half-years

So:

```text
A = P (1 + 5/100)^3
```

(Use only if asked.)

## 📝 6. Practice Questions (with Answers)

```text
1)  Find SI on ₹6000 at 10% p.a. for 4 years.
2)  Find CI on ₹5000 at 8% p.a. for 2 years (compounded annually).
3)  SI on a sum at 12% p.a. for 3 years is ₹2160. Find principal.
4)  At what rate will a sum of ₹4000 amount to ₹5200 in 3 years at SI?
5)  Find the difference between CI and SI on ₹8000 at 10% p.a. for 2 years.
6)  Find the amount on ₹10,000 at 20% p.a. for 2 years compounded annually.
7)  Principal = ₹9000, R = 10% p.a., T = 1 year. Find SI and CI.
8)  In how many years will ₹5000 become ₹8000 at SI at 12% p.a.?
9)  A sum becomes ₹6724 in 2 years at CI at 10% p.a. Find principal.
10) If CI for 2 years on a sum at 5% p.a. is ₹512.5, find principal.
```

---

### ✅ Answers (Brief)

1. SI:

```text
SI = 6000×10×4 /100 = 6000×40/100 = 2400
```

→ **₹2400**, Amount = 8400

2. CI, P=5000, R=8%, T=2:

```text
A = 5000(1.08)^2 = 5000×1.1664 = 5832
CI = 5832 −5000 = 832
```

→ CI = **₹832**

3. SI = 2160, R=12%, T=3:

```text
2160 = P×12×3/100 = P×36/100
P = 2160×100/36 = 216000/36 = 6000
```

→ **₹6000**

4. P=4000, A=5200, T=3:

SI = 5200 −4000 = 1200

```text
1200 = 4000×R×3/100
1200 = 120R
R = 10%
```

→ **10% p.a.**

5. Difference CI−SI 2 years:

```text
= P(R/100)^2 = 8000×(10/100)^2
= 8000×1/100 = 80
```

→ **₹80**

6. P=10000, R=20%, T=2:

Factor: 1.2² = 1.44

```text
A = 10000×1.44 = 14400
CI = 4400
```

→ Amount **₹14400**, CI **₹4400**

7. P=9000, R=10%, T=1:

- SI = 9000×10×1/100 = 900
- CI (1 year) = same as SI = 900

→ **SI = CI = ₹900**

8. 5000 → 8000 at 12% SI:

SI = 8000 − 5000 = 3000

```text
3000 = 5000×12×T/100
3000 = 600T
T = 3000/600 = 5 years
```

9. A=6724, R=10%, T=2:

Factor 1.1² = 1.21

```text
6724 = P×1.21
P = 6724/1.21 = 5560
```

→ P = **₹5560**

10. CI 2 years, R=5%, CI=512.5

Use factor: (1.05)² = 1.1025

Let P = x:

```text
A = x×1.1025
CI = A − x = 512.5
x(1.1025 −1) = 512.5
x×0.1025 = 512.5
x = 512.5 / 0.1025 = 5000
```

→ P = **₹5000**

> 2–3 saal wale CI ko **year-wise** karne ki aadat daalo –
