## 🔑 Core Definitions (Bilkul Clear)

- **Cost Price (CP)** – Jitne me cheez kharidi (basic cost).
- **Selling Price (SP)** – Jitne me cheez bechi.
- **Profit (Gain)** –  
  SP &gt; CP → Profit = SP − CP
- **Loss** –  
  SP &lt; CP → Loss = CP − SP

- **Profit %**:

```text
Profit % = (Profit / CP) × 100
```

- **Loss %**:

```text
Loss % = (Loss / CP) × 100
```

> [!TIP] 💡  
> Base **hamesha CP** hota hai – profit/loss CP ke upar calculate hota hai.

---

## 📐 Standard Relations – CP, SP, MP, Discount

### 1️⃣ Profit / Loss Formulas

If Profit % = P:

```text
SP = CP × (100 + P) / 100
CP = SP × 100 / (100 + P)
```

If Loss % = L:

```text
SP = CP × (100 − L) / 100
CP = SP × 100 / (100 − L)
```

> [!MEMORY] 💡  
> **“Plus for Profit, Minus for Loss”** – denominator me always 100.

---

### 2️⃣ Marked Price (MP) &amp; Discount

- **Marked Price (MP)** – tag price, jispe “discount” dikhaya jata hai.
- **Discount**:

```text
Discount = MP − SP
Discount % = (Discount / MP) × 100
SP = MP × (100 − Discount%) / 100
```

## 🧮 Algorithms / Stepwise Templates

### 🔹 Template A – Plain Profit/Loss (CP, SP, %) Problems

1. Identify known: CP/SP/P%/L%  
2. If % known → convert to multiplier:
   - Profit P% → SP = CP × (1 + P/100)
   - Loss L% → SP = CP × (1 − L/100)
3. Rearrange to find missing.

#### Example (Level 1)

CP = 600, Profit % = 20% → SP?

SP = 600 × 1.2 = **₹720**

---

### 🔹 Template B – Discount + Profit Together

Type:

> After giving x% discount on MP, shopkeeper still gains y% profit. Find MP.

Steps:

1. Assume CP = 100 (MI: **Make It 100** trick).  
2. SP = 100 × (1 + y/100).  
3. SP = MP × (1 − x/100).  
4. Equate SPs, find MP.

#### Example (Level 2)

After giving 10% discount, shopkeeper still gets 20% profit. CP = 450. Find MP.

Here CP actual = 450.

Step 1: Find SP from profit:

SP = 450 × 1.2 = 540

Step 2: SP = MP × (1 − D/100) = MP × 0.9

0.9 MP = 540 → MP = 540 / 0.9 = **₹600**

---

### 🔹 Template C – Successive Discount

Two discounts d₁% and d₂% on MP.

Net discount %:

```text
Net % = d₁ + d₂ − (d₁d₂ / 100)
```

(“minus” kyon? Kyunki dono effect compounding se kam ho jata hai.)

#### Example (Level 2)

10% and 20% discount on MP = 1000. Find SP.

Net discount:

```text
= 10 + 20 − (10×20)/100
= 30 − 2
= 28%
```

SP = 1000 × (1 − 0.28) = 1000 × 0.72 = **₹720**

---

### 🔹 Template D – Cost Increase but Same Profit %

Type:

> Cost increases by x%, shopkeeper wants to keep same profit %. By what % should he increase SP?

Steps:

1. Assume CP_old = 100.  
2. SP_old = 100 × (1 + profit%)  
3. New CP = 100 × (1 + x/100)  
4. New SP = new CP × (1 + profit%)  
5. Compare SPs → % increase in SP.

#### Example (Level 2)

Cost up by 10%, profit = 20% maintained. % increase in SP?

Old: CP=100, SP=120  
New: CP=110, SP=110×1.2=132  
% increase in SP = (132−120)/120 ×100= 10%

## 🎯 Levels of Questions

### LEVEL 1 – Foundation

#### Q1

CP = 80, SP = 100, Profit %?

Profit = 20  
Profit % = 20/80×100 = 25%

---

#### Q2

CP = 400, Loss = 10%, SP?

SP = 400 × 0.9 = **₹360**

---

### LEVEL 2 – Exam Typical

#### Q3 – Find CP from SP &amp; Profit %

SP = 720, Profit = 20%. Find CP.

SP = CP × 1.2 → CP = 720 / 1.2 = **₹600**

---

#### Q4 – Successive Discount

MP = 800, discounts 10% then 20%.

Net discount:

```text
= 10 + 20 − (10×20)/100
= 30 − 2
= 28%
```

SP = 800 × 0.72 = **₹576**

---

#### Q5 – Loss % Case

CP = 600, loss = 25%, SP?

SP = 600 × 0.75 = **₹450**

---

### LEVEL 3 – High-Level / Mains Style Patterns

#### Pattern 1 – Profit % on SP vs CP Confusion

**Q6.** A shopkeeper sells an article at 20% profit on **selling price**. What is the profit % on cost price?

Let SP = 100.  
Profit = 20% of SP = 20.  
So CP = SP − Profit = 100 − 20 = 80.

Now profit % on CP:

```text
= Profit / CP ×100
= 20/80×100 = 25%
```

✅ 25%

**Shortcut Pattern:**

If profit is x% **on SP**, then on CP:

```text
Profit% (on CP) = 100x / (100 − x)
```

(for x = 20 → 2000/80 = 25%)

---

#### Pattern 2 – Find CP from Profit on Two Parts

(typical Mains statement):

> A shopkeeper sells an article at 20% profit. If he had sold it for ₹40 more, he would have gained 30%. Find CP.

Let CP = x.

Given:

- 20% profit SP₁ = 1.2x  
- 30% profit SP₂ = 1.3x  

Difference SP₂ − SP₁ = 40:

```text
1.3x − 1.2x = 40
0.1x = 40
x = 400
```

CP = **₹400**

---

#### Pattern 3 – Marked Price with Two Layers

> A shopkeeper marks his goods 40% above CP and gives 20% discount on MP. Find overall profit %.

Assume CP = 100.

MP = 140  
Discount 20% → SP = 140×0.8 = 112

Profit = 112 −100 = 12 → 12%

So overall profit % = **12%**

## ⚠️ Common Traps &amp; Mistakes

1. **Profit % base galat**

   - Profit/Loss % hamesha **CP ke respect me** hota hai,  
     jab tak clearly “on SP” na likha ho.

2. **Discount ke baad direct percent sochna**

   - “40% above CP, 20% discount” ko 40 −20 = 20% net mat samjho.  
   - Actual net = 12% hi hota hai (as above example shows).

3. **Successive % ko simple add/subtract karna**

   - 10% discount, phir 20% discount → 30% nahi, 28% net.
