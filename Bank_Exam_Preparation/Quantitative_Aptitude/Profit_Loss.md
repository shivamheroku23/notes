# 💰 Profit &amp; Loss – Exam-Oriented Shortcuts

> 🧠 Used everywhere in bank exams: word problems, DI, mains-level caselets.

---

## 🌟 1. Basic Terms (Bilkul Simple)

- **Cost Price (CP)** – Jitne me cheez kharidi.
- **Selling Price (SP)** – Jitne me cheez bechi.
- **Profit (Gain)** –  
  SP &gt; CP → Profit = SP − CP
- **Loss** –  
  SP &lt; CP → Loss = CP − SP

---

## 📌 2. Important Formulas

### 2.1 Profit / Loss %

```text
Profit % = (Profit / CP) × 100
Loss %   = (Loss / CP) × 100
```

### 2.2 SP &amp; CP Relations

If Profit % = P:

```text
SP = CP × (100 + P) / 100
CP = SP × 100 / (100 + P)
```

If Loss % = L:

```text
SP = CP × (100 − L) / 100
CP = SP × 100 / (100 − L)
```

> [!MEMORY] 💡  
> **Plus for Profit, Minus for Loss**  
> (Always with 100 in denominator.)

---

## 🧠 3. Marked Price &amp; Discount

- **Marked Price (MP)** – Printed price (tag pe likha hota).
- **Discount** – MP se kam me bechna.

```text
Discount = MP − SP
Discount % = (Discount / MP) × 100
SP = MP × (100 − Discount%) / 100
```

---

## 📘 4. Solved Examples (Step-by-Step)

### Example 1 – Simple Profit %

A shopkeeper buys a toy for ₹80 and sells it for ₹100. Find profit %.

Profit = SP − CP = 100 − 80 = 20

```text
Profit % = (20 / 80) × 100
         = (1/4) × 100
         = 25%
```

✅ Profit % = **25%**

---

### Example 2 – Find SP from Profit %

CP = 600, Profit % = 20%. Find SP.

```text
SP = CP × (100 + P) / 100
   = 600 × 120 / 100
   = 600 × 1.2
   = 720
```

✅ SP = **₹720**

---

### Example 3 – Find CP from Profit %

A man sold a book for ₹180 at 20% profit. Find CP.

We know:

```text
SP = CP × (100 + P) / 100
180 = CP × 120 / 100
CP = 180 × 100 / 120
   = 180 × (5 / 6)
   = 150
```

✅ CP = **₹150**

---

### Example 4 – Discount

MP = ₹200, discount = 10%. Find SP.

```text
SP = MP × (100 − D) / 100
   = 200 × 90 / 100
   = 200 × 0.9
   = 180
```

✅ SP = **₹180**

---

### Example 5 – Successive Discounts

A shopkeeper offers 10% and then 20% discount on MP ₹1000. Find final SP.

Use successive % formula:

```text
Net % change = −10 −20 + (−10×−20)/100
             = −30 + 2
             = −28% (28% total discount)
```

So total discount 28%:

```text
SP = MP × (100 − 28) / 100
   = 1000 × 72 / 100
   = 720
```

✅ Final SP = **₹720**

---

## 🎯 5. Cost Increase vs Profit %

### Example 6

A shopkeeper wants to maintain 20% profit. Cost increases by 10%. By what % should he increase SP?

Use successive % idea:

Let old CP = 100, then old SP (20% profit) = 120.

Now CP increases by 10% → new CP = 110.

To keep 20% profit → new SP = 110 × 1.2 = 132.

% increase in SP:

```text
= (132 − 120)/120 ×100
= 12/120 ×100
= 10%
```

✅ SP must increase by **10%**.

> [!TIP] ⚡  
> Assume CP = 100 in % problems. It makes calculations **very easy**.

---

## 🧠 6. Memory Tricks

- “**Plus for Profit, Minus for Loss**”  
  SP = CP × (100 ± profit/loss%) / 100
- For quick %:
  - 10% = divide by 10  
  - 20% = ×2 of 10%  
  - 25% = one-fourth  
  - 50% = half

## 📝 7. Practice Questions (with Answers)

```text
1)  CP = ₹250, SP = ₹300. Find profit %.
2)  CP = ₹400, loss = 10%. Find SP.
3)  SP = ₹720, profit % = 20%. Find CP.
4)  MP = ₹500, discount = 12%. Find SP.
5)  MP = ₹800, discount 10% then 20%. Find final SP.
6)  A man buys an article for ₹600 and sells it at 25% loss. Find SP.
7)  A trader wants 30% profit. If CP = ₹800, what should be SP?
8)  After giving 10% discount, a shopkeeper still gets 20% profit. If CP = ₹450, find MP.
9)  A cycle is sold for ₹3600 at 20% loss. Find CP.
10) On selling an article for ₹840, a shopkeeper gains 40%. Find CP.
```

---

### ✅ Answers (Brief)

1. CP 250 → SP 300  
   Profit = 50  
   Profit % = 50/250 ×100 = 20% → **20%**

2. Loss 10% → SP = 400 × (100 −10)/100 = 400 × 90/100 = **₹360**

3. SP = 720, Profit 20%  

```text
CP = SP × 100 / 120
   = 720 × (5/6)
   = 600
```

→ **₹600**

4. SP = 500 × 88/100 = 440 → **₹440**

5. Successive discount 10% then 20%:

```text
Net % = −10 −20 + 2 = −28%
SP = 800 × 72/100 = 576
```

→ **₹576**

6. 25% loss:

```text
SP = 600 × 75/100 = 450
```

→ **₹450**

7. 30% profit:

```text
SP = 800 × 130/100 = 1040
```

→ **₹1040**

8. Let MP = x, discount 10%:

```text
SP = 0.9x
CP = 450, profit 20% → SP = 450×1.2 = 540
So, 0.9x = 540 → x = 540/0.9 = 600
```

→ MP = **₹600**

9. SP 3600 at 20% loss:

```text
SP = CP × 80/100
CP = 3600 × 100/80 = 3600×1.25 = 4500
```

→ **₹4500**

10. SP 840 at 40% profit:

```text
SP = CP × 140/100
CP = 840 × 100/140 = 840×(10/14)= 840×(5/7)= 600
```

→ **₹600**
