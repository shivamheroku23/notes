# 🚣 Boats &amp; Streams – Speed with Water Flow

> 🧠 Ye chapter Time–Speed–Distance ka extension hai.  
> Bas **stream (water current)** ke effect ko dhyaan me rakhna hai.

---

## 🌊 1. Key Terms

- **Still water** – paani bilkul stable, no flow.
- **Stream / Current** – paani ka flow (usually downstream direction).
- **Downstream** – boat + water flow **same direction**.
- **Upstream** – boat direction **against** water flow.

---

## 🌟 2. Basic Formulas

Let:

- Boat speed in still water = B
- Stream speed = S

Then:

```text
Downstream speed = B + S
Upstream speed   = B − S
```

From downstream &amp; upstream speed, we can find:

```text
B = (Downstream + Upstream)/2
S = (Downstream − Upstream)/2
```

> [!TIP] 💡  
> “Plus for downstream, minus for upstream”.

---

## 🧮 3. Solved Examples

### Example 1 – Find B &amp; S

A boat goes downstream at 12 km/hr and upstream at 8 km/hr. Find speed of boat in still water and speed of stream.

Use formulas:

```text
B = (Down + Up)/2 = (12 + 8)/2 = 20/2 = 10 km/hr
S = (Down − Up)/2 = (12 − 8)/2 = 4/2 = 2 km/hr
```

✅ Boat speed = 10 km/hr, Stream speed = 2 km/hr

---

### Example 2 – Time to Travel

Boat speed in still water = 15 km/hr, stream speed = 3 km/hr.  
Time to travel 36 km upstream?

First find upstream speed:

```text
Upstream speed = B − S = 15 − 3 = 12 km/hr
```

Time = Distance / Speed = 36/12 = **3 hours**

---

### Example 3 – Downstream Time Given

A man can row a boat 20 km downstream in 2 hours and the same distance upstream in 4 hours. Find speed in still water and stream speed.

Downstream speed = 20/2 = 10 km/hr  
Upstream speed = 20/4 = 5 km/hr  

Now:

```text
B = (10 + 5)/2 = 15/2 = 7.5 km/hr
S = (10 − 5)/2 = 5/2 = 2.5 km/hr
```

✅ Boat = 7.5 km/hr, Stream = 2.5 km/hr

## 📝 4. Practice Questions (with Answers)

```text
1)  A boat goes downstream at 18 km/hr and upstream at 10 km/hr.
    Find the speed of the boat in still water and the speed of the stream.

2)  A boat can travel 30 km downstream in 3 hours and the same distance upstream in 5 hours.
    Find the speed of the boat in still water.

3)  A boat can travel 24 km upstream in 4 hours.
    If the speed of the stream is 2 km/hr, find the speed of the boat in still water.

4)  A man can row at 12 km/hr in still water.
    If the stream’s speed is 3 km/hr, how much time will he take to row 30 km downstream?

5)  A man rows 18 km downstream in 2 hours and 12 km upstream in 3 hours.
    Find the speed of boat in still water and the speed of the stream.
```

---

### ✅ Answers (Brief)

1) Down = 18, Up = 10

```text
B = (18 + 10)/2 = 28/2 = 14 km/hr
S = (18 − 10)/2 = 8/2 = 4 km/hr
```

2) Downstream = 30/3 = 10, Upstream = 30/5 = 6

```text
B = (10 + 6)/2 = 16/2 = 8 km/hr
```

3) Upstream speed = 24/4 = 6 km/hr

```text
Upstream = B − S = 6, S = 2 → B = 6 + 2 = 8 km/hr
```

4) Downstream speed = 12 + 3 = 15 km/hr

```text
Time = 30/15 = 2 hours
```

5) Downstream = 18/2 = 9, Upstream = 12/3 = 4

```text
B = (9 + 4)/2 = 13/2 = 6.5 km/hr
S = (9 − 4)/2 = 5/2 = 2.5 km/hr
```
