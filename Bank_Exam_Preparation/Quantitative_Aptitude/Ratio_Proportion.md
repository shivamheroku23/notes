# ⚖️ Ratio &amp; Proportion – Base of Many Topics

> 🧠 Ratio–Proportion ke bina Percentage, Mixture, Partnership, DI sab adhura hai.

---

## 🌟 1. Ratio – Very Simple

**Ratio** compares **two quantities** of same type.

Example:

- Boys : Girls = 3 : 2
- Means if total 5 equal parts:
  - Boys = 3 parts
  - Girls = 2 parts

> [!NOTE] 📌  
> 3 : 2 does **not** mean exactly 3 boys, 2 girls;  
> it means **in that proportion**.

---

### 1.1 Converting Ratio to Actual Values

If Total = T and ratio = a : b, then:

```text
Sum of parts = a + b
Value of 1 part = T / (a + b)
First value = a × (T/(a+b))
Second value = b × (T/(a+b))
```

### Example 1

In a class, ratio of boys to girls is 3 : 2. Total students = 40. Find boys &amp; girls.

Sum of parts = 3 + 2 = 5

1 part = 40 / 5 = 8

- Boys = 3 × 8 = 24
- Girls = 2 × 8 = 16

✅ Boys = 24, Girls = 16

## 🌐 2. Proportion

When two ratios are **equal**:

```text
a : b = c : d   → a/b = c/d
```

Then they are in **proportion**.

Also:

```text
a : b = c : d  → a × d = b × c
```

This is called **cross multiplication**.

### Example 2

Check if 2 : 3 and 4 : 6 are in proportion.

2/3 = 0.666…, 4/6 = 0.666… → yes.

Or:

2 × 6 = 12  
3 × 4 = 12 → equal → yes.

---

## 🔁 3. Important Ratio Rules (Fast)

### 3.1 Multiplying / Dividing

If you multiply or divide **all terms** by same number, ratio doesn’t change.

3 : 5 = (3×2) : (5×2) = 6 : 10

---

### 3.2 Adding Same Quantity

If same quantity is added/subtracted to **both actual values**, ratio **changes**.

Example:

- A : B = 3 : 5  
- A = 30, B = 50  
- Add 10 to both → A = 40, B = 60 → new ratio = 2 : 3

---

## 🧮 4. Concept of “Parts” (Very Important)

Always think ratio in terms of **parts**.

Example:

- Ratio of ages of A and B is 4 : 5.  
  After 10 years, ratio becomes 6 : 7. Find present ages.

Let current ages = 4x, 5x.

After 10 years:

- A = 4x + 10
- B = 5x + 10

Given:

```text
(4x + 10) / (5x + 10) = 6 / 7
Cross multiply:
7(4x + 10) = 6(5x + 10)
28x + 70 = 30x + 60
10 = 2x
x = 5
A = 4x = 20
B = 5x = 25
```

✅ Present ages: 20, 25

## 📘 5. Direct &amp; Inverse Proportion

### 5.1 Direct Proportion

If one increases, other also increases:

- More hours worked → more money
- More speed → more distance in same time

```text
a ∝ b → a1/b1 = a2/b2
```

### 5.2 Inverse Proportion

If one increases, the other **decreases**:

- More men → less days to finish work
- More speed → less time for same distance

```text
a ∝ 1/b → a1×b1 = a2×b2
```

### Example 3 – Inverse

8 workers complete a work in 15 days. In how many days will 10 workers complete it?

Workers × Days = constant

8 × 15 = 10 × D  
120 = 10D → D = 12

✅ 10 workers → **12 days**

---

## 🧠 6. Memory Tricks

- **Ratio = Parts** – Always convert total into “parts”.
- **Direct → divide** (a1/b1 = a2/b2)  
- **Inverse → multiply** (a1×b1 = a2×b2)

## 📝 7. Practice Questions (with Answers)

```text
1)  Ratio of A and B is 3 : 4. If A = 24, find B.
2)  In a class, ratio of boys to girls is 5 : 3. Total students = 64. Find boys &amp; girls.
3)  Find x, if 4 : x = 12 : 21.
4)  A and B share profit in ratio 2 : 3. If total profit is ₹5000, find their shares.
5)  A:B:C = 2:3:5 and total = 100. Find each share.
6)  12 men can finish a work in 18 days. How many days will 9 men take? (Assume inverse proportion)
7)  If 5 pens cost ₹75, how much will 8 pens cost? (Assume direct proportion)
8)  The ratio of ages of P and Q is 4 : 7. After 6 years, their ages will be 10 : 15. Find their present ages.
9)  In a mixture of milk and water, ratio is 3 : 1. If total is 80 litres, find milk and water.
10) If 2x : 3x = 8 : 12, find x.
```

---

### ✅ Answers (Brief)

1. A:B = 3:4, A=24:

1 part = 24/3 = 8 → B = 4×8 = **32**

2. 5:3, total 64:

Sum = 8, 1 part = 64/8 = 8  
Boys = 5×8 = 40, Girls = 3×8 = **24**

3. 4/x = 12/21 → cross-multiply:

4×21 = 12x → 84 = 12x → x = 7

4. Ratio 2:3, total 5000:

Sum = 5, 1 part = 1000  
A = 2×1000 = 2000, B = 3×1000 = **3000**

5. 2:3:5, total 100:

Sum = 10, 1 part = 10  
A=20, B=30, C=50

6. 12 men × 18 days = 9 men × D

216 = 9D → D = 24 days

7. 5 pens → 75, 1 pen = 15, 8 pens = 8×15 = 120

8. Present ages = 4x, 7x

After 6 years: (4x+6)/(7x+6) = 10/15 = 2/3

3(4x + 6) = 2(7x + 6)  
12x + 18 = 14x + 12  
6 = 2x → x=3

P = 4x=12, Q = 7x=21

9. Milk : Water = 3:1, total 80:

Sum =4, 1 part=20  
Milk=60, Water=20

10. 2x:3x = 8:12

2x/3x = 8/12 = 2/3  
LHS already 2/3 for any x (x≠0) → **any non-zero x** satisfies. (Usually such questions meant to simplify, not solve.)
