# ⚖️ Ratio &amp; Proportion – Core Engine for Arithmetic &amp; DI

> 🧠 Ratio–Proportion clear ho gaya, to Percentage, Mixture, Partnership, Time–Work, DI sab aadha asaan ho jata hai.  
> Yaad: Ratio = **parts thinking**, Proportion = **equal ratio**, Direct/Inverse = **saath-saath / ulta relation**.

---

## 🧭 Concept Map – Where Ratios Are Used?

- Mixture &amp; Alligation (milk–water, cheap–dear)
- Partnership (profit sharing)
- Time &amp; Work (men–days, efficiency comparisons)
- Time &amp; Speed &amp; Distance (speed ratios)
- DI (when table me sirf ratio diya ho, exact values nahi)

Isliye ye chapter **foundation** hai.

---

## 🌟 1. Ratio – Core Idea

**Ratio** compares **two similar quantities** as parts.

Example:

- Boys : Girls = 3 : 2  
  → Total 5 equal parts → Boys = 3 parts, Girls = 2 parts.

> [!NOTE] 📌  
> 3 : 2 ka matlab “exactly 3 and 2” nahi hota, balki  
> **kisi bhi common factor ke saath** same ratio ho sakta hai (30:20, 15:10, 6:4…).

---

### 1.1 Convert Ratio to Actual Values

If total = T and ratio = a : b:

```text
Total parts = a + b
1 part      = T / (a + b)
First value = a × (T / (a + b))
Second value= b × (T / (a + b))
```

#### Example 1 (Level 1)

Boys : Girls = 3 : 2, total students = 40.

Total parts = 3+2 = 5  
1 part = 40/5 = 8

- Boys = 3×8 = 24  
- Girls = 2×8 = 16

---

### 1.2 Three-Part Ratios

If A:B:C = 2:3:5, total T:

```text
Total parts = 2+3+5 = 10
1 part = T/10
A = 2×T/10; B = 3×T/10; C = 5×T/10
```

## 🌐 2. Proportion &amp; Cross Multiplication

When two ratios are equal:

```text
a : b = c : d  →  a/b = c/d
```

Then:

```text
a × d = b × c
```

(isko cross-multiplication kehte hain – equation checking / solving ke kaam aata hai)

#### Example 2

Check 2:3 and 4:6

2×6 = 12, 3×4 = 12 → equal → in proportion.

---

## 🧱 3. Ratio Rules &amp; Transformations

### Rule 1 – Multiply/Divide All Terms

Ratio same rehta hai:

- 3 : 5 = (3×2) : (5×2) = 6 : 10

---

### Rule 2 – Addition/Subtraction on Actual Values

**If same number add/subtract to both actual values, ratio change ho jata hai.**  
(This point often used in age/salary problems.)

Example:

- A = 30, B = 50 → A:B = 3:5  
- Add 10: A=40, B=60 → 2:3

---

## 🧩 4. “Parts” Thinking – Very Important

Hamesha ratio ko **parts** me socho.

### Example – Ages

Ratio of ages of A and B is 4 : 5. After 10 years, ratio becomes 6 : 7. Find present ages.

Let:

- A = 4x, B = 5x

After 10 years:

- A = 4x + 10
- B = 5x + 10

Given:

```text
(4x + 10)/(5x + 10) = 6/7
7(4x + 10) = 6(5x + 10)
28x + 70 = 30x + 60
10 = 2x
x = 5
A = 20, B = 25
```

## 🔁 5. Direct &amp; Inverse Proportion

### 5.1 Direct Proportion (Saath-saath badhe/ghate)

If one increases, other also increases in **same ratio**:

- Jaise zyada hours kaam → zyada paisa
- Zyada speed → same time me zyada distance

Mathematically:

```text
a ∝ b → a1/b1 = a2/b2
```

#### Example – Direct

5 pens → ₹75, 8 pens ka cost?

1 pen = 75/5 = 15  
8 pens = 8×15 = ₹120

Ya direct:

5:8 = 75:X → cross-multiply → 5X = 600 → X=120

---

### 5.2 Inverse Proportion (Ulta Relation)

If one increases, other decreases (product constant):

- Zyada men → kam din
- Zyada speed → kam time (same distance)

```text
a ∝ 1/b → a1×b1 = a2×b2
```

#### Example – Inverse (Level 1)

8 workers complete work in 15 days. 10 workers kitne din?

Workers×Days constant:

8×15 = 10×D → 120 = 10D → D = 12 days

## 🎯 Levels of Questions

### LEVEL 1 – Basics

1) Ratio of A:B = 3:4, A = 24, B?

1 part = 24/3 = 8 → B = 4×8 = 32

---

2) Boys : Girls = 5:3, total = 64.

Total parts = 8, 1 part=8  
Boys = 5×8=40, Girls=3×8=24

---

### LEVEL 2 – Typical Exam-Type

#### Q3 – Age Ratio Change

P:Q = 4:7. After 6 years, 10:15. Find present ages.

Present: 4x, 7x

After 6 years:

```text
(4x+6)/(7x+6) = 10/15 = 2/3
3(4x+6) = 2(7x+6)
12x+18 = 14x+12
6 = 2x → x=3
P = 12, Q=21
```

---

#### Q4 – Profit Sharing (Partnership Style)

A and B share profit in ratio 2:3. Total profit = 5000. Find shares.

Total parts = 5, 1 part=1000  
A = 2×1000=2000  
B = 3×1000=3000

---

### LEVEL 3 – High-Level / Twisted

#### Q5 – Complex Ratio Change

The ratio of two numbers is 3:5. If 6 is added to both, ratio becomes 4:5. Find numbers.

Let numbers = 3x and 5x.

After +6:

```text
(3x+6)/(5x+6) = 4/5
5(3x+6) = 4(5x+6)
15x+30 = 20x+24
30−24 = 20x−15x
6 = 5x
x = 6/5 = 1.2
Numbers = 3x = 3.6 and 5x = 6
```

Agar integer required ho to data coherent hoga; pattern samajhna important hai:  
**same addition/subtraction → ratio equation banana.**

---

#### Q6 – Direct vs Inverse Mixed

12 men can complete a work in 15 days, working 6 hours per day. In how many days will 9 men, working 8 hours per day, complete the work?

Total work ∝ (men × days × hours)

Let W be work.

Case 1:

```text
W ∝ 12×15×6
```

Case 2: Let days = D:

```text
W ∝ 9×D×8
```

Equate:

12×15×6 = 9×D×8

Simplify:

- 12 and 9: divide by 3 → 4×15×6 = 3×D×8  
- 4×15×6 = 360  
- RHS = 24D  
→ 360 = 24D → D = 15

So answer = **15 days**

## 🧠 Memory / Pattern Notes

- “Ratio = Parts” – total ko parts me tod do, har term ko uske parts assign karo.
- Direct proportion → **divide** (a1/b1 = a2/b2)  
- Inverse proportion → **multiply** (a1×b1 = a2×b2)
- Same addition/subtraction in numerator/denominator → equation se x solve karo.

## 📝 Practice Set (with Answers)

```text
1)  Ratio of A:B = 7:5. If A = 42, find B.
2)  Income of A and B are in ratio 3:4. If total income is ₹56,000, find their incomes.
3)  Two numbers are in ratio 5:7. If their sum is 72, find the numbers.
4)  The ratio of ages of a father and son is 7:3. After 10 years, ratio will be 9:5. Find their present ages.
5)  12 men can finish a work in 18 days. How many days will 9 men take? (same hours per day)
6)  If 6 machines can produce 180 units in 5 days, how many units will 8 machines produce in 3 days? (direct proportion)
7)  In a mixture of milk and water, ratio is 3:1. If total is 80 litres, find milk and water.
8)  A sum is divided in ratio 2:5:3 among X, Y, Z. If total is ₹40,000, find each share.
9)  If 4:x = 12:21, find x.
10) The ratio of two numbers is 4:5. If each number is increased by 10, ratio becomes 5:6. Find the numbers.
```

---

### ✅ Answers (Brief)

1) 7:5, A=42 → 1 part=6 → B=5×6=30  
2) 3:4, total=56000 → parts=7 → 1 part=8000 → A=24000, B=32000  
3) 5:7, sum=72 → parts=12 → 1 part=6 → numbers=30,42  
4) Present 7x,3x; after 10: (7x+10)/(3x+10)=9/5 → solve → x=5 → Father=35, Son=15  
5) 12×18 = 9×D → D=24 days  
6) Units ∝ machines×days:  
   6×5→180 → 1 machine-day=6 units  
   8 machines×3 days=24 machine-days → 24×6=144 units  
7) 3:1, total=80 → parts=4, 1 part=20 → milk=60L, water=20L  
8) 2:5:3, total=40000 → parts=10, 1 part=4000 → X=8000, Y=20000, Z=12000  
9) 4/x = 12/21 → 4×21 = 12x → 84=12x → x=7  
10) 4x,5x; plus 10: (4x+10)/(5x+10)=5/6 → solve to get numbers (you can verify as practice).
